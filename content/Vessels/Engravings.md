#Vessel #Lore 
**Engravings** are what can be described as the living scribes of one's journey, imprints that record pacts, trials, and more. These engravings carve new channels into the [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage), bestowing powers that can be both subtle and profound. Whether born of ancient [Relics](Relics), the gift of [Glyph Grafts](Glyph%20Grafts), or the experience of [Pathmarks](Pathmarks), each engraving is a testament to an encounter that reshaped the bearer's being. As a vessel accumulates these marks, it becomes a tapestry of the owner's history. However, this also means each engraving can shift the balance of a vessel; intertwining these gifts comes with risks, as they can place a heavy strain on a vessel. Thus, proper caution must be exercised when making use of these engravings.

Not all engravings are permanent; the main two examples are those from [Attunement](Attunement) (which applies to relics as well), as untuning from the relevant things results in the corresponding engravings going with them.
