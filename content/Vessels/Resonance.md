#Vessel #Lore 
# Overview
Some Vessels bear the imprint of ancestors whose history continue to shape their lineage, bargains with certain entities, or some other means of special birth and childhood. These are **Resonances** and they typically lie dormant until either stress or development awakens them: a child of a storm-caller lineage my summon lightning in a moment of fatal terror, a scion of necromancers might hear the dead whisper to them, or a child of draconic lineage may begin to display physical traits of their **Resonance** as they age. Such innate gifts grant shortcuts to power, but are also able to bind their bearers to their **Resonance**, especially as there are some that come as a result of a deal or pact of sorts. Additionally, while broad examples have been provided already, some **Resonances** may occur in the form of specific abilities or the innate aptitudes for the ways and techniques of a lineage. 

Many families that carry such lineages guard these legacies and their lines, as those with a **Resonance** feel an enhanced need to preserve the **Resonance** for the future. It is not known exactly the origin of the psychological effect of these **Resonances** beyond the running theory that, as they affect one's [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage), they affect one's self as well.
