#Vessel #Lore 
# Overview
Some [[Vessel Homepage|Vessels]] bear the imprint of ancestors whose history continue to shape their lineage, bargains with certain entities, or some other means of special birth and childhood. These are **Resonances** and they typically lie dormant until either stress or development awakens them: a child of a storm-caller lineage my summon lightning in a moment of fatal terror, a scion of necromancers might hear the dead whisper to them, or a child of draconic lineage may begin to display physical traits of their resonance as they age. Such innate gifts grant shortcuts to power, but are also able to bind their bearers to their resonance, especially as there are some that come as a result of a deal or pact of sorts. Additionally, while broad examples have been provided already, some resonances may occur in the form of specific abilities or the innate aptitudes for the ways and techniques of a lineage. 

Many families that carry such lineages guard these legacies and their lines, as those with a resonance feel an enhanced need to preserve the resonance for the future. It is not known exactly the origin of the psychological effect of these resonances beyond the running theory that, as they affect one's vessel, they affect one's self as well.
