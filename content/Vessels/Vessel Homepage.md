# Overview
Since time immemorial, the world has been composed of seven fundamental currents, each a note in the grand symphony of Aleria's energies. These [Essentias](Essentias) were born after what is recognized as the [The Melody](The%20Melody), when the raw components of Aleria began to separate and became defined: breath coalescing into [Mana](Mana), lands formed that gave birth to nature and [Prana](Prana), and the spark of self-awareness that ignited [Vitus](Vitus) within every emerging form. The lingering of those forms congealed into [Eidon](Eidon), the memory of lives past. More complex thought itself hammered into reality [Eidos](Eidos). Decay's first whisper became [Miasma](Miasma). From fear bore both [Divinus](Divinus) and [Inferis](Inferis), etched entirely of faith and blood. Together, these fonts of essentias underlie every spell, every miracle, every phenomenon, and every dream. They demand both reverence and restraint, for their raw forms rend flesh and spirit asunder, allowing few to interact with these energies directly.

# The Vessel
While unknown in origin, every soul manifests in a singular, mystical structure known as a vessel, a metaphysical, private crucible to provide middle ground between flesh, spirit, and essentias. Though imperceptible to mundane senses, vessels bear intricate signatures in relation to the identity of their owner: some may pulse with a gentle, opalescent light beneath the skin, others can flicker with tempestuous eddies.

At birth, a vessel is an empty container, waiting to be infused. In uncommon cases, infants emerge with vessels pre-saturated by ancestral, divine, elemental, or other forces, usually marking them for destinies with certain essentias; this is known as [Resonance](Resonance). Now, just as some are born with [Harmonics](Harmonics), there are those born with [Dissonance](Dissonance), where there is something amiss with their vessel. The most common of these cases would be those who are born without a vessel, known as the [Hollowed](Dissonance#The%20Hollowed%20\(Born%20Broken\)). Those born with neither may undergo a form of Harmonics, which are rituals performed typically by someone highly proficient with their abilities and type of essentias. These rituals result in cleaning the ambient energies within the vessel to allow for one main type of essentia. This process must be done at a young age, as its difficulty increases exponentially and one is left with a vessel too contaminated with a myriad of essentia to allow for either cleansing or use.

A mature vessel performs three vital tasks: containment, regulation, and expression of essentias. It filters their raw power while shaping each practitioner's style, limits, and vulnerabilities. As such, who someone is as a person affects the way their vessel may interact with their relevant essentia, allowing for some abilities to come more naturally to them than others. The more one uses these energies, the more their vessel's is mapped for it, allowing progression in one's mastery. Of course, those who recklessly force foreign essentias into their vessel risk catastrophic [Fracture](Dissonance), resulting in devastating effects on those foolish or foolhardy enough to dare attempt such a feat.

# Multiple Essentias
In recent years, it has become more common to see people with more than one type of essentia, though it is still considered rare. These people are known as practitioners of [Synchronicity](Synchronicity). Throughout history, there have always been those few who displayed mastery of multiple types without degradation in any of them. The secrets behind these individuals are ill-studied and often left a mystery. This issue is further assisted by the fact that there are many who would wish to learn these secrets for themselves, so those who can do so in a proper way typically hide such a fact.

As for the reason there has been an increase in these individuals, it is a result of [The Janitor Association](The%20Janitor%20Association.md). This association provides a means (that remains unknown) to those who are [Janitors](Janitors) at a high financial cost. While janitors that have undergone this process are able to wield more than one type of essentia without risk, this is noted to have the effect that it comes with reduced proficiency when compared to the original. It is believed that their method results in a vessel that now holds "chambers." However, these "chambers" mean that the vessel's full capability is now split amongst essentias, rather than the harmony and expertise showcased by true users of multiple essentias.
