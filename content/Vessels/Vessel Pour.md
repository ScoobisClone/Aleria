# Overview
**Vessel Pour** is the art of fully expressing one's [[Vessel Homepage|Vessel]] upon the world. While most applications of [[Essentias|Essentia]] involve drawing from the Vessel and directing its contents toward a particular purpose, a Pour instead requires the practitioner to open the Vessel itself and allow its nature to be expressed outwardly. It is considered the pinnacle of [[Abstraction]], as this moves on from manipulating the contents of one's vessel to expressing the Vessel as an extension of one's being.

No two Vessel Pours are the same. Even individuals with similar Abstractions may produce radically different Pours, as the final expression is shaped not only by the nature of the Vessel but also by the person who possesses it. For this reason, Vessel Pour is less a singular technique than it is a broad principle through which an individual gives their Vessel from within reality. 

There are several recognized forms of Vessel Pour, though these classifications serve to describe the manner in which a Vessel is expressed rather than its strength. A single Pour may belong to multiple classifications, and possessing more classifications does not inherently make a Pour more powerful.

# Categories
### Imprint
An **Imprint** is a Vessel Pour in which the practitioner expresses their Vessel across a defined area, temporarily imposing aspects of themselves upon the surrounding world. The resulting space becomes an extension of the practitioner's Vessel, with its environment and magical phenomena reflecting their nature.

An Imprint may alter the behavior of Essentia, the properties of the environment, or even the rules by which certain phenomena function within its boundaries. The exact nature of these changes varies entirely between practitioners, with some creating elaborate realms while others produce little more than a subtle alteration of reality.

Because the practitioner is effectively expressing their Vessel across the space itself, Imprints are often regarded as the closest equivalent to a personal realm.

### Infusion
An **Infusion** is a Vessel Pour in which the practitioner expresses their Vessel through an object. Unlike ordinary enchantment, an Infusion does not simply place an effect upon an object before allowing it to return to its original state. The object is fundamentally altered by the experience, at minimum taking on some physical or symbolic characteristic of its creator's Vessel.

These changes may be permanent even after the Pour has ended. However, the true power of an Infusion is generally only realized when its creator pours their Vessel through the object once more. In such cases, the object acts as a medium through which the practitioner's Vessel can be expressed with considerably greater definition.

### Manifestation
A **Manifestation** is a Vessel Pour in which the practitioner's Vessel takes the form of a distinct entity or entities. These may be a creature, construct, avatar, familiar, or other independent expression capable of acting separately from its creator.

The resulting manifestation does not necessarily possess a mind of its own. Some are little more than extensions of their creator's will, while others may possess complex behavior or even a high degree of independence. What defines a Manifestation is simply that the Vessel has been given an external form capable of existing apart from the practitioner. This is not to be confused with an [[Avatar]].

### Transfiguration
A **Transfiguration** is a Vessel Pour in which the practitioner expresses their Vessel through their own body, temporarily transforming themselves into a more complete manifestation of what their Vessel represents.

Unlike [[Vessel Burst]], which forces the contents of the Vessel through the body to empower it, Transfiguration is an outward expression of the Vessel upon the self. The practitioner does more than simply become physically stronger; their body, abilities, and manner of interacting with the world are altered to reflect their Vessel.

The resulting form is unique to the practitioner and may range from subtle alterations to an almost complete transformation.

### Convergence
A **Convergence** is a Vessel Pour in which the practitioner's Vessel is expressed in direct union with something outside of itself. Rather than simply changing an object, environment, or creature, the Vessel and the external subject become intertwined, allowing their properties to influence one another.

The subject of a Convergence may be another person, an object, a source of Essentia, a magical phenomenon, or even another manifestation of power. The resulting expression is therefore dependent upon both sides of the union and may produce something that neither could achieve independently.

Unlike Infusion, which primarily changes the thing receiving the Vessel, Convergence requires the external subject to remain an active part of the resulting expression.

### NOTE
These classifications are not considered exhaustive. As understanding of Vessel Abstraction continues to develop, scholars have proposed additional forms of Pour that do not fit neatly within the existing classifications. This is unsurprising, as attempting to categorize Vessel Pour is ultimately an attempt to categorize the innumerable ways in which an individual may express themselves through their Vessel.
