A **Dissonance** is an abnormal state of the [[Vessel Homepage|Vessel]]. The term denotes a deviation from ordinary Vessel function, not necessarily a defect in the conventional sense. Some Dissonances are debilitating; others confer capabilities unavailable to an unaffected practitioner. The cause, manifestation, and severity of a Dissonance vary between individuals. A diagnosis should therefore be based upon the underlying abnormality rather than the presence of any single symptom. The observations recorded below reflect established manifestations where available. Matters that remain uncertain identified as such.

## The Hollowed
#### Cause
The Hollowed are individuals born with Vessels incapable of properly regulating or containing their [[Essentias|Essentia]] or lack one altogether. The precise origin of this defect remains undetermined. In affected individuals, Essentia passes through improperly formed channels, placing continuous, heavy strain upon the body and soul. The condition is congenital and, in the majority of documented cases, fatal.
#### Symptoms
Affected individuals exhibit severe physiological and spiritual distress from birth. Organ failure, uncontrolled Essentia discharge, and progressive deterioration are common. Most perish before surviving infancy can be reasonably expected. 

The rare survivors are known as the Tempered. These individuals frequently demonstrate extraordinary physical resilience, heightened perception of Essentia, and capabilities exceeding ordinary physiological limits. Such adaptations do not indicate that the underlying defect has been corrected.

Soulthirst is a recurring condition among the Tempered. It manifests as an intense craving for the sensation of Essentia passing through the body. Affected individuals may consume enchanted objects, absorb unrefined currents, or deliberately expose themselves to dangerous sources of Essentia, such as offensive spells. The severity varies, though the compulsion can become sufficiently pronounced to threaten the individual's survival.
#### Treatment
No reliable method of correcting the congenital defect has been established. Treatment is primarily concerned with preserving the individual's stability and limiting the consequences of uncontrolled Essentia circulation. The extraordinary resilience of a Tempered individual should never be mistaken for recovery.

Management of Soulthirst varies according to severity. The long-term effectiveness of any particular intervention remains insufficiently established.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis for a Hollowed individual is poor. Most succumb to the initial effects of the condition. Those who survive may develop remarkable adaptations, but remain subject to the consequences of their abnormal Vessel. Survival should not be interpreted as evidence of a favorable overall prognosis.

## The Sundered
#### Cause
The Sundered acquire their condition through structural damage to the Vessel. Excessive strain, catastrophic misuse of Essentia, or severe external trauma may tear its channels and permanently compromise its function.

The precise nature of the damage varies. Some injuries disrupt the circulation of particular currents; others interfere with the Vessel's ability to regulate or direct Essentia. In all confirmed cases, the Vessel retains sufficient structural unity to continue functioning as a single whole, though not necessarily as an effective one.
#### Symptoms
Manifestations depend upon the location and severity of the injury. Affected individuals may experience interrupted Essentia circulation, diminished control, abnormal expenditure of power, or unintended effects when employing familiar techniques.

Some manifestations are particularly counterintuitive. Essentia may behave contrary to its intended application, producing effects that the bearer neither desires nor can reliably prevent. Such cases are frequently mistaken for failures of technique. Repeated examination is advised before incompetence is concluded.
#### Treatment
Structural damage of this nature cannot reliably be resolved through ordinary recovery. Treatment is directed toward stabilizing the damaged Vessel and compensating for lost functionality.

Some practitioners pursue Grafting: the introduction of an external source of power or functionality into the compromised Vessel. Materials and sources used in this process include parasitic spirits, foreign Essentia cores, and in one confirmed case, fragments of Divinity. Results vary considerably.

A successful graft may restore a degree of practical function. It does not necessarily repair the original damage. Rejection, dependency, and further instability remain significant risks.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis depends upon the extent of the injury and the effectiveness of subsequent intervention. Some individuals retain useful control over their Essentia despite permanent impairment. Others experience progressive deterioration or become dependent upon external sources to sustain their capabilities.

Grafting may improve practical function, but no general conclusion regarding its long-term safety can presently be justified.
#### Progression
Further catastrophic damage may result in a [[#The Shattered|Shattered]] Vessel. This outcome is not inevitable and should not be assumed merely because the original injury is severe.

## The Lost
#### Cause
The Lost are individuals whose soul has lost its anchor to the world. The precise mechanism responsible for this failure remains uncertain. Available observations suggest that the condition involves a fundamental disruption in the relationship between the soul, body, and Vessel, though the order in which these failures occur has not been conclusively established.

The condition is exceptionally severe. It should not be diagnosed solely on the basis of ordinary Vessel instability or physical deterioration.
#### Symptoms
The Lost exhibit profound instability of physical existence. Their bodies may dissolve and reform repeatedly, while their continued presence in the material world appears increasingly tenuous. Those who encounter them frequently describe the individuals as wraithlike, though this description is observational rather than diagnostic.

A persistent attraction to sources of Essentia is also characteristic. This compulsion, commonly termed the Vague Hunger, drives the Lost toward concentrations of power regardless of their suitability or accessibility.

The underlying purpose of this behavior is unknown. Essentia may provide temporary stability, sustain the remnants of the individual's existence, or produce some other effect that has yet to be determined. No reliable evidence establishes that the hunger can be permanently satisfied.
#### Treatment
No reliable method of restoring the lost anchor has been established. The ability to delay physical dissolution or temporarily stabilize the individual should not be interpreted as a cure.

Interventions involving Essentia may alter the observable symptoms, but their long-term effectiveness remains unverified.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis is grave. The Lost are subject to persistent instability and an apparently insatiable compulsion toward Essentia. Whether their condition can be reversed remains unknown.

The absence of a documented recovery must not be treated as proof of impossibility, as the absence of evidence is not the evidence of absence. It is, however, sufficient reason to regard any proposed treatment with considerable caution.

## The Corrupted
#### Cause
The Corrupted possess Vessels whose internal Essentia has been tainted by an abnormal influence. Contamination may originate from tainted [[Miasma]], corrupted expressions of [[Inferis]], foreign Essentia, or the residue of another compromised source. The effects depend upon the contaminant and the Vessel into which it is introduced.

The precise mechanism by which contamination becomes established is not uniform across documented cases. It is therefore inadvisable to assume that every instance of corruption shares a single origin or responds to the same intervention.
#### Symptoms
The defining symptom is a persistent alteration in the behavior of the affected Essentia. Familiar techniques may produce unintended or inverted effects. Healing may inflict harm, protective workings may become hostile, and currents ordinarily employed for creation may instead produce decay.

Physical manifestations are variable. Some individuals exhibit discoloration, blackened veins, abnormal growths, or deterioration of nearby Essentia. Others show no obvious external symptoms despite demonstrable abnormalities in their output.

The severity of the condition cannot be reliably judged by appearance alone.
#### Treatment
Treatment requires identification of the contaminant wherever possible. Measures intended to suppress the resulting symptoms may be of limited value if the source of contamination remains active.

The removal or neutralization of a taint cannot be assumed to restore the Vessel to its former condition. Prolonged exposure may produce lasting alterations in the affected currents, and continued use of contaminated Essentia may exacerbate the condition.

No universal treatment is known to be effective against all forms of internal tainting.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis varies according to the nature of the contaminant, the extent of its influence, and the effectiveness of intervention. Some individuals retain considerable control over their abilities, while others experience progressively less predictable manifestations.

The acquisition of unusual capabilities following contamination should not be taken as evidence that the condition is stable or beneficial. The long-term consequences of a particular taint must be assessed independently.

## The Echoed
#### Cause
The Echoed develop when a Vessel splinters in a manner that disrupts the consciousness associated with it. A Vessel is intimately connected to the essence, identity, and soul of its bearer. Damage to its structure may therefore affect more than the circulation of Essentia. In certain cases, the fracture extends into the practitioner's sense of self, producing distinct reflections of their consciousness.

The precise conditions required for this phenomenon are not fully understood. Not every damaged Vessel produces such effects, and the relationship between the extent of structural damage and the severity of the resulting symptoms remains uncertain.
#### Symptoms
The principal manifestation is a disruption in the continuity of identity. The affected individual may experience conflicting intentions, inconsistent recollections, abrupt emotional changes, or difficulty maintaining a coherent understanding of themselves.

In some cases, distinct aspects of the individual's consciousness become sufficiently pronounced to be identified as Echoes. Each may emphasize a particular memory, conviction, emotion, or interpretation of the self. One may preserve a cherished memory while another rejects it; one may express compassion while another is consumed by resentment.

These Echoes should not automatically be regarded as separate souls or wholly independent persons. Current understanding suggests that they represent aspects of the same individual rendered discordant by the splintering of the Vessel.

Severe cases may involve competition between Echoes for control of the same body. The affected individual may display marked changes in behavior, recollection, or magical expression according to which Echo is most prominent.

The use of Essentia may also become inconsistent. Different Echoes may exhibit distinct preferences or proficiencies, resulting in variations in the practitioner's techniques and manifestations. Such differences do not, by themselves, establish the existence of separate Vessels.
#### Treatment
Treatment is directed toward restoring coherence between the affected aspects of the individual's consciousness. Suppression of an Echo is not necessarily beneficial and may exacerbate the underlying discord.

Where possible, interventions should aim to establish stable communication and cooperation between the fragments rather than treating every manifestation as an independent adversary. The suitability of this approach varies with the individual and the severity of the condition.

No reliable method of restoring the Vessel's original unity has been established. Improvement in the individual's functioning should not be assumed to indicate complete resolution of the underlying fracture.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis varies considerably. Some Echoed individuals retain substantial independence and can manage their condition with appropriate support. Others experience persistent conflict, impaired recollection, or an unstable sense of identity that significantly interferes with daily function.

The extent to which the original unity of the consciousness can be restored remains uncertain. Cases of successful reconciliation warrant continued study, but should not be treated as evidence of a universally applicable treatment.

## The Shattered
#### Cause
The Shattered possess Vessels that have lost their structural unity. The condition typically follows overwhelming force or catastrophic misuse, resulting in the fragmentation of the Vessel's original organization. Its channels no longer maintain the relationships required for reliable circulation of Essentia.

The distinction between structural fragmentation and ordinary damage is significant. In a Shattered individual, the problem is not merely that particular channels have been injured. The Vessel can no longer reliably function in any way.

#### Symptoms
Essentia circulation becomes irregular and difficult to predict. Power may accumulate in one portion of the Vessel while failing to reach another, causing techniques to fluctuate between extraordinary potency and complete failure.

Practitioners may find that spells collapse before completion, prayers lose their intended effect, or manifestations break apart into uncontrolled surges. Such events can occur despite adequate knowledge and preparation. The failure lies in the Vessel's ability to sustain the required flow, rather than necessarily in the practitioner's technique.

The severity of the symptoms depends upon the remaining connections between fragments and the extent to which the Vessel can still direct its contents.
#### Treatment
Treatment is directed toward stabilizing the remaining structure and improving the reliability of Essentia circulation. Rituals, external conduits, and other interventions may reconnect isolated streams or reduce the unpredictability of the Vessel's output.

These measures do not necessarily restore the original organization. A practitioner may regain a degree of control while retaining a permanently fragmented Vessel.

Attempts to force the Vessel into its former patterns of circulation should be approached with caution. The original structure may no longer be capable of supporting them.

#### Prognosis
The prognosis depends upon the extent of fragmentation and the effectiveness of stabilization. Some individuals retain limited but useful control over their Essentia. Others remain subject to severe fluctuations that make reliable practice difficult or impossible.

Complete restoration of the original Vessel cannot presently be assumed. Any claim of successful reconstruction requires evidence that the Vessel has regained coherent function, rather than merely producing a temporary improvement in output.

## The Veiled
#### Cause
The Veiled possess Vessels in which a particular current or function has been deliberately sealed away. The seal may be self-imposed, established through ritual, or imposed by an external force. Its purpose and consequences depend upon the nature of the restriction.

The sealed current is not necessarily destroyed or contaminated. Rather, the Vessel has been rendered unable to access it through its ordinary functions. The degree to which the inaccessible current remains present within the underlying structure is not always readily determined.
#### Symptoms
The defining symptom is the inability to access the sealed current or function. Attempts to employ it may produce no response, an incomplete manifestation, or adverse effects if the seal is unstable.

The remaining functions of the Vessel may operate without significant impairment. In some cases, restriction allows the accessible currents to develop with unusual precision or efficiency. A practitioner may consequently demonstrate exceptional specialization despite the permanent loss of a portion of their former capabilities.

Such advantages are not guaranteed. The effect of a seal depends upon what has been restricted and how the remaining Vessel accommodates the loss.
#### Treatment
Where the seal is voluntary, its removal requires the bearer's informed participation and an understanding of the original purpose of the restriction. Where it is imposed externally, the means of removal depend upon the method by which it was established.

The weakening or removal of a seal should not be attempted without consideration of the Vessel's current state. Prolonged restriction may have altered the way its remaining functions operate, and the return of an inaccessible current may disrupt that balance.

A seal that can be removed is not necessarily one that should be removed without preparation.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis is highly variable. Some Veiled individuals maintain stable and effective Vessels indefinitely, while others suffer significant impairment from the restriction or its instability.

The condition is not inherently detrimental. Its consequences must be assessed according to the capabilities sacrificed, the effects upon the remaining Vessel, and the circumstances under which the seal was imposed.

## The Starved
#### Cause
The Starved possess Vessels unable to obtain or sustain sufficient usable Essentia for their ordinary functions. The condition may arise through external deprivation, deliberate restriction, disruption of access to available sources, or an abnormality that prevents the Vessel from drawing in or retaining an adequate supply.

These mechanisms are not identical, though they produce a common result: the quantity of usable Essentia available to the bearer falls below what the Vessel requires.
#### Symptoms
Initial symptoms may include fatigue, diminished physical performance, impaired concentration, and reduced control over Essentia. As deprivation deepens, techniques that once required little effort may become exhausting, while demanding applications may become impossible to sustain.

Prolonged deprivation can threaten the stability of the Vessel itself. Repeated attempts to draw upon insufficient reserves may place additional strain upon the bearer, while desperate efforts to obtain Essentia may expose them to unsuitable or hazardous sources.

The severity of the condition depends upon the degree of deprivation, its duration, and the amount of Essentia required to sustain the individual's ordinary functions.
#### Treatment
Treatment is directed toward restoring an adequate supply of usable Essentia and addressing the cause of the deprivation. Where access to external sources has been restricted, the restoration of that access may be sufficient to improve the condition. Where the Vessel itself cannot draw in or retain Essentia, additional intervention may be required.

The indiscriminate consumption of enchanted objects or unrefined Essentia is not a reliable treatment. Such measures may provide temporary relief while introducing further risks to an already compromised system.

The source of deprivation should be identified wherever possible. Restoring access without resolving an underlying defect may produce only temporary improvement.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis depends upon the cause, severity, and duration of deprivation. Individuals whose access to Essentia is restored before lasting damage occurs may recover much of their ordinary function. Prolonged or severe cases may result in persistent impairment.

The degree of physical deterioration alone is not a reliable measure of the Vessel's remaining capacity. Assessment should include the bearer's ability to obtain, retain, and use Essentia under stable conditions.

## The Overgrown
#### Cause
The Overgrown possess Vessels in which a particular current or function has developed beyond the limits required to maintain ordinary equilibrium. The cause of this disproportionate expansion varies and is not fully understood. It is most frequently observed in individuals with a pronounced affinity for a particular current, though such an affinity is not, by itself, sufficient to establish the condition.

The defining abnormality is not simply an abundance of Essentia. It is the excessive development of one aspect of the Vessel relative to the rest, resulting in an imbalance that the structure struggles to regulate.
#### Symptoms
The dominant current frequently exhibits capabilities beyond the practitioner's ordinary limits. Individuals strongly attuned to Prana may become living nexuses of natural forces or spirits, while those attuned to Eidon may experience an overwhelming presence of ancestral influences. The precise manifestations depend upon the current involved and the nature of the bearer.

As the imbalance develops, the Vessel may become increasingly dependent upon its dominant current. Other functions may diminish in relative importance or become difficult to sustain, and attempts to employ unfamiliar or incompatible currents may destabilize the bearer's control.

In severe cases, the loss of equilibrium can produce abrupt fluctuations in Essentia circulation or a collapse of the Vessel's ability to regulate its contents. Such events are not inevitable, but their possibility should be considered when assessing the risks of continued use.
#### Treatment
Treatment is directed toward stabilizing the Vessel's internal balance. Depending upon the nature of the condition, this may involve limiting the dominant current, regulating its use, or developing methods through which the remaining functions can accommodate its expansion.

Attempts to suppress the dominant current without understanding the extent of the Vessel's dependence upon it may prove dangerous. In advanced cases, the current may have become integral to the Vessel's existing function, and abrupt restriction can produce further instability.

No universal method of reversing this has been established. Treatment must be based upon the particular configuration of the affected Vessel.
#### Prognosis
The prognosis varies with the extent of the imbalance and the practitioner's ability to maintain stable circulation. Some Overgrown individuals retain considerable control and derive substantial benefit from their expanded capabilities. Others become increasingly dependent upon their dominant current and vulnerable to disruptions that the Vessel can no longer readily accommodate.

The presence of extraordinary power is not a reliable indicator of stability. The relevant consideration is whether the Vessel can continue to regulate its functions without allowing its dominant aspect to compromise the whole.

_The classifications above are intended to describe observed states of the Vessel. They should not be interpreted as exhaustive accounts of every possible manifestation, nor as guarantees of a particular outcome. Individual assessment remains necessary. A diagnosis that accounts for the name of a condition but not its actual presentation is of limited practical value. As such, do not contact me regarding failed treatment without proper diagnosis._
