#Vessel #Lore 
# Overview
For those unshackled by [Resonance](Resonance), a form of deliberate attunement comes as a result of **Harmonics**. **Harmonics** are rituals that allow for the preparation and subsequent alignment of a [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) to a chosen [Essentia](Essentias). While they can be performed at later ages, it is preferable to be done at a young age (though not too young as to allow one's vessel to develop), as a [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) may become muddied by various [Essentia](Essentias) it takes in passively. Allowing the buildup of clashing and interaction of these [Essentia](Essentias) as one ages progressively makes it more and more difficult to "clean" up a [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) for **Harmonics**. Eventually it reaches a point of being nigh impossible to complete a **Harmonic** for all but the most proficient and precise.

The preparation phase in **Harmonics** is to clean out the [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) to purify it. As mentioned previously, it is ideal to do so when there is still little cleaning to be done. While extremely rare, the goal is a complete 100% cleaning that removes all other [Essentia](Essentias) except for the target type. In most cases, a proper **Harmonic** is expected to reach a minimum of 80% cleaning for those that are developed into late childhood. Once this preparation is complete, the individual undergoing **Harmonics** begins to have their [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) attuned to the [Essentia](Essentias) as determined by the individual who is conducting the ritual. Upon completion, the [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) in question now holds an alignment and capability for an [Essentia](Essentias).

Now, these **Harmonics** can vary from type to type, group to group, and even individual to individual: an Alchemist might forge a brew into a stew capable of providing the alignment phase, druids may take more ceremonial rituals to coax Prana into every bit of one's [[Vessel Homepage|Vessel]], or a psychic may even attempt to brute force the process in a moment of haste (effectively blasting the poor recipient). Regardless of the method, the end result of **Harmonics** should be an unlocked and even enhanced affinity. 

Earlier, it was mentioned that a [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) should be allowed to develop before performing a **Harmonic**. This is a result of how the rituals may strain a [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage). Not all rituals accept all individuals, resulting in a strain beyond design. As a result, a [Vessel](Vessel%20Homepage) may fall into [Dissonance](Dissonance) at best or result in a complete annihilation of the individual at worst.
