#Vessel #Lore 
# Overview
For those unshackled by [Resonance](Resonance), a form of deliberate attunement comes as a result of **Harmonics**. Harmonics are rituals that allow for the preparation and subsequent alignment of a [[Vessel Homepage|Vessel]] to a chosen [[Essentias|Essentia]]. While they can be performed at later ages, it is preferable to be done at a young age (though not too young as to allow one's vessel to develop), as a vessel may become muddied by various essentia it takes in passively. Allowing the buildup of clashing and interaction of these essentia as one ages progressively makes it more and more difficult to "clean" up a vessel for harmonics. Eventually it reaches a point of being nigh impossible to complete a harmonic for all but the most proficient and precise.

The preparation phase in harmonics is to clean out the vessel to purify it. As mentioned previously, it is ideal to do so when there is still little cleaning to be done. While extremely rare, the goal is a complete 100% cleaning that removes all other essentia except for the target type. In most cases, a proper harmonic is expected to reach a minimum of 80% cleaning for those that are developed into late childhood. Once this preparation is complete, the individual undergoing harmonics begins to have their vessel attuned to the essentia as determined by the individual who is conducting the ritual. Upon completion, the vessel in question now holds an alignment and capability for an essentia.

Now, these harmonics can vary from type to type, group to group, and even individual to individual: an Alchemist might forge a brew into a stew capable of providing the alignment phase, druids may take more ceremonial rituals to coax Prana into every bit of one's vessel, or a psychic may even attempt to brute force the process in a moment of haste (effectively blasting the poor recipient). Regardless of the method, the end result of harmonics should be an unlocked and even enhanced affinity. 

Earlier, it was mentioned that a vessel should be allowed to develop before performing a Harmonic. This is a result of how the rituals may strain a vessel. Not all rituals accept all individuals, resulting in a strain beyond design. As a result, a vessel may fall into [Dissonance](Dissonance) at best or result in a complete annihilation of the individual at worst.
