# Mechanics and Examples


# Overview
**Vessel Burst** is the art of forcing the contents of one's [[Vessel Homepage|Vessel]] through the body in a sudden and overwhelming release. Where [[Vessel Pour]] expresses the Vessel outwardly upon the world, a Burst turns that expression inward, allowing the practitioner to temporarily embody the nature of their Vessel.

The process is often compared to releasing pressure from a sealed container. Rather than carefully drawing [[Essentias|Essentia]] through the body's normal channels, the practitioner opens the Vessel and allows its contents to flood through them at once. The resulting surge can dramatically enhance the practitioner's physical abilities, mystical capabilities, or other qualities associated with their Vessel. Despite the name, a Vessel Burst does not physically transform the practitioner. Their body remains fundamentally unchanged, with the effects instead being expressed through what the body is capable of doing. In this regard, a Burst is an expression of the Vessel through the self rather than a transformation of the self.

Unlike Vessel Pour, Vessel Burst does not require any form of [[Abstraction]]. Any practitioner may theoretically perform one, provided they possess sufficient control over their Vessel to survive the release. However, an ordinary Vessel places considerable limitations upon what can be expressed through the body. Those with Abstraction tend to possess a greater understanding of their Vessel and can therefore produce more deliberate, potent, and complex Bursts.

A Burst is, to clarify, not simply an increase in the amount of [[Essentias|Essentia]] being used. The effect is shaped by the nature of the practitioner's Vessel and their understanding of themselves. Elaborating on the prior mentioned boosts, a person gains a variety of benefits and boosts based on the person. The expression itself varies greatly as the individuals who perform it. 

There is one peculiar phenomenon universally associated with Vessel Burst. While a practitioner is Bursting, music can often be faintly head around them. The music has no apparent physical source and differs from person to person, seemingly reflecting something about the individual and their Vessel. Some practitioners produce little more than a barely perceptible melody, while others have been known to fill an entire room with music loud enough to be clearly heard by everyone nearby. The music cannot be intentionally selected or controlled, and its significance remains unknown.

Regardless, the primary danger of Vessel Burst is the strain it places upon the mind. The body is forced to contain and direct an amount of Essentia far beyond what it normally handles, requiring the practitioner to maintain control while their Vessel effectively floods their entire being. Prolonged use can result in exhaustion, loss of concentration, [[Dissonance]], and more. For this reason, a Burst is generally short-lived. The practitioner must release the accumulated pressure before their body or vessel can no longer contain it. Those who attempt to maintain a Burst beyond their limits risk losing control of the Essentia entirely, causing the release to become increasingly destructive to both the practitioner and their surroundings.
