## Description
### Manifestation
The Last Witness appears a tall, gaunt humanoid figure draped in a tattered, shifting fabric that is akin to that of twilight and shadow. Its face is perpetually obscured by an ash-like veil, though those who have glimpsed beneath the veil describe seeing a myriad of eyes (with the exact number changing between different accounts) arranged in no discernible pattern. Its fingers are unnaturally long and end in points that leave trails of fading light in the air when its fingers move or it gestures.

Around the Astrali is a perpetual radius of approximately 33 feet in which sound becomes muffled, colors appear desaturated, and living beings within the radius become more aware of the passing of time. While these effects are only noticeable when inside the radius, the radius itself may be spotted by an outside observer via the small motes of light that drift upward like embers.

### Behavior
The [[Astrali Homepage|Astrali]] tends to disappear and remanifest itself in nearby locations where nearby deaths are soon to occur, which applies to the deaths of animals and not just humanoids. It then stands silently at the periphery, observing with its many eyes, and does not intervene regardless of what transpires before it. Upon being approached, it does not flee or attack initially. It continues to maintain stillness. If attacked or approached further, it will phase into an incorporeal state and relocate to a nearby point in which it may continue its observations. Prolonged exposure to its presence causes those nearby to experience vivid visions of loss, usually personal in some manner like the loss of a loved one. 

### Personality
While not malevolent, it appears to be profoundly dethatched. It has been noted to speak rarely and only ever in response to direct questions. Based on current information, it possesses a vast memory of deaths across history, with there being statements that imply the inclusion of deaths across planes as well.

It shows curiosity toward those who have survived near-death experiences, often lingering near them longer than usual. To these individuals, it occasionally offers cryptic statements, such as "You were not meant to be there." The full extent of the intention behind these statements is not known as of now.

## Research Notes
### Discovery
The Last Witness was first documented during the founding of [[Forn]] in the year ???, when multiple soldiers reported a "tall shadow" watching from the edges of a particularly brutal siege. Initial reports were dismissed as a form of mass hysteria due to exhaustion, but the frequency of sightings and the consistency of descriptions prompted [[The Third Accord]] to investigate. 

A research team was dispatched to the region, and after three weeks of observation, they made contact successfully. The Astrali did not react aggressively to their presence, instead turning its attention toward the researchers with a noticeable curiosity. The first recorded words from The Last Witness were: "They will find you in time, as they find all things."

### Accord Notes
- The Last Witness appears to be culturally universal, as there are loose references to a figure akin to it throughout most of every civilization's mythology. The Last Witness denies any such titles and has claimed to only be passing along. 
- It has been observed in multiple planes simultaneously before, indicating either multiple instances or an ability to project itself to other planes. As of now, it is not known if they are different or all share one consciousness.
- While it does not actively harm researchers, its passive effect of inducing visions has led to psychological distress amongst personnel, and possible breaks amongst junior personnel. Senior researchers are recommended for extended contact.
- The Astrali has shown no interest in Gifts or Extractions, neither offering them freely nor reacting negatively to them when prompted.

### Containment Procedures
Containment of The Last Witness is considered impractical and potentially counterproductive. The current protocol involves:
1. Monitoring known manifestation sites.
2. Stationing senior observation teams at sites containing The Last Witness with rotation schedules as to minimize psychological strain.
3. Maintaining open communication channels to document new sightings.
4. Deploying a response team if the Astrali manifests in populated areas with high civilian density, to guide civilians away from its field and document its observations.

## Hazard Profile
### Containment Rating Explanation
The following properties have allowed The Last Witness to be classified as Shuten:
- Incorporeal Phasing: Can become intangible at will, passing through solid matter and avoiding physical containment measures.
- Planar Locomotion: Observed to shift between planes of existence.
- Passive Disruption: Presence causes psychological distress and sensory distortion.

### Danger Rating Explanation
The Last Witness holds a Danger Rating of 06 as a result of the following:
- Psychological Hazard: Prolonged exposure causes visions that can result in attempted suicide in extreme cases.
- Passive Area Effect: Holds a field that causes disorientation amongst those in it, especially in regards to their sense of time.
- Memory Alteration: Researchers have reported difficulty in distinguishing their own memories and feelings from those that originate from the vision.
- Incorporeal Phasing: Can become intangible at will, passing through most attacks.

### Abilities
- Death-Vision Inducement: Those within The Last Witness' field experience visions of past deaths, both personal and historical. These visions are realistic and can cause disorientation. 
- Incorporeal Phasing: The Astrali can become intangible, passing through matter and magical barriers.
- Planar Shifting: It can move between planes of existence at will, disappearing from one location and appearing in another.
- Cognizance of Endings: The Last Witness possesses perfect recall of all deaths within its vicinity and can sense impending deaths before they occur.
- Temporal Distortion: Time seems to slow or speed up within its presence field, with observed dilation ranging between .5 to 1.5 seconds per second experienced.
- Silence Aura: Sound becomes muffled within its field, making communication difficult.

### Containment Notes
- Containment is not recommended. Resources are better allocated to observation and documentation. Should one of the instances be contained, it will eventually disappear and reappear elsewhere in the world.

### Suppression Notes
- The Last Witness is not aggressive and does not require suppression. Attempt to attack or drive it away have resulted in its relocation rather than any defensive response.
- [[The Janitor Association]] has made two attempts to capture the Astrali for extraction purposes; both attempts failed due to it relocating itself.
- Deterrence has proven ineffective to prevent it from making use of its planar shifting capabilities.

### Accord Casualty Notes
- Direct Encounters: 2 deaths, 23 cases of severe psychological trauma.
- Indirect Effects: 12 deaths associated with disorientation during disasters.

# Gifts
## Gift Name
**Classification:** E02

**Form:**
A small, handheld mirror with a dark, obsidian-life surface. The frame appears to be made of twisted, silver-grey metal that resembles frozen smoke. The mirror's surface does not reflect as a normal mirror should. Rather, it shows the last moments of those who look into it. Should it already be determined, it would show their own death, otherwise it shows the death of the nearest person who was died within the last year.

**Effect:**
When activated, the mirror shows a vision of a death that has already occurred. The user can choose to view the last moments of a specific person. (if the user knows their name and has a connection to them) or allow the mirror to show the most significant recent death in the area. Significance is determined by how many are aware of the death.

The visions are of great clarity, granting the user insight into the circumstances of the death, the emotional state of the dying individual, and any last words or thoughts.

**Requirements:**
- The user must have experienced a significant loss within their lifetime.
- The Gift can only be used once per day.
- Repeated use may cause the user to experience mild psychological symptoms.

# Extractions
## Offensive Extraction
**Classification:** E04
**Type:** Variable

**Form:**
A long, slender knife with a blade that seems to absorb light, appearing as a void of black in the shape of a blade. The handle is wrapped in leather that feels like frozen skin, and a small, eye-like gem is embedded in the pommel. The weapon is balanced for throwing more for throwing than close-quarters.

**Effect:**
When the blade strikes a living target, it briefly induces a vision in the target, stunning them for 1-3 seconds while they experience the sensation of dying. This effect does not inflict actual damage, but it can disorient targets.

Against already-wounded targets, the blade can "read" the target's wounds and find the most efficient path to fatality, guiding the user's attacks toward vital areas. This effect is subtle and requires the user to be aiming properly.

# Erosion
### Initial Use
The user experiences a slight sense of detachment, a feeling of watching their own life as an observer rather than a participant. They may find themselves becoming introspective and more thoughtful about mortality and endings. This is generally considered a minor effect, akin to mild melancholy.

### Stressed
While stressed, the user's visions become more vivid and intrusive. They may see their own death at inconvenient moments, or the deaths of those around them. This can cause hesitation and second-guessing. The user's ability to focus on the present degrades.

### Panic
In states of panic, the user's perception blurs as they see everyone's deaths, past, present, and future, bleeding into the present moment. This is incapacitating; they cannot distinguish what is real from what is vision. This state may lead to self-harm or complete shutdown as the user becomes overwhelmed by the sheer sensory overload.

### Full Erosion
The user's consciousness is overwritten by The Last Witness' perception of reality. They become a passive observer, no longer acting, only watching. Their body remains functional, but their personality and memories are subsumed by the Astrali's detached awareness. Physically, the user's eyes become dark and numerous as small eyes begin to form across their skin, especially on their face and hands. The user also grows more lankier and begins to have their skin become an ash-like color as clothes akin to those worn by The Last Witness sprouts from their skin before separating partially.
