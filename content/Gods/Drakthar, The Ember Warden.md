# NOTE: This is a test template for Gods
> "A flame kept is warmth. A flame neglected is ruin."

Drakthar is the god of fire, security, and safety, embodying the dual nature of flame. Fire is not inherently benevolent nor destructive; it is a tool whose consequences depend upon the hands that tend it. A controlled flame warms a home, hardens steel, cooks food, and illuminates the darkness. The same flame, left unattended, can consume everything it was meant to protect.

Drakthar therefore teaches that strength exists to protect, power exists to be controlled, and every useful flame must have someone willing to tend it.

He is traditionally depicted as a broad, heavily armored figure bearing a great shield engraved with burning runs and a hammer whose head constantly smolders. His armor is often depicted as blackened steel with lowing seams, resembling a forge that has never completely cooled.

His worshippers, known as the [[Embered Faithful]], commonly include blacksmiths, soldiers, guards, builders, watchmen, and anyone responsible for protecting others.

---

# Divine Principles
Drakthar's faith is built around three principles:

### **Fire Must Be Tended**
Power left unattended becomes dangerous. Followers are expected to maintain the things entrusted to them, whether that means a household hearth, a weapon, a fortress, or another person's safety.

### **Strength Must Protect**
The strong are not forbidden from using their strength. They are expected to use it responsibly. Drakthar considers strength wasted when it exists solely for domination or vanity.

### **Creation Requires Destruction**
Fire destroys, but destruction is not inherently evil. Old metal must be melted before it can become something new. A dead tree can become fuel. A failed structure may need to be demolished before something safer can replace it. One must distinguish destruction with purpose from destruction born of negligence or malice.

---

# Edicts
Followers of Drakthar are expected to:
- Protect those entrusted to your care.
- Maintain and repair tools, weapons, structures, and places of shelter.
- Keep dangerous forces properly contained.
- Aid those endangered by fire, disaster, or structural collapse.
- Use strength and authority to establish stability rather than fear.
- Create things that improve the safety or prosperity of others.
- Extinguish dangerous flames before they can spread.
- Keep important fires, forges, and places of refuge maintained.

# Anathema
Drakthar considers the following acts anathema:
- Knowingly endanger others through negligence.
- Abandon someone under your protection without good reason.
- Deliberately allow a preventable disaster to occur.
- Destroy a useful creation solely for spite.
- Use fire indiscriminately against innocents or protected structures.
- Deliberately sabotage tools, defenses, shelters, or infrastructure relied upon by others.
- Hoard knowledge necessary to prevent an imminent disaster.
- Leave a dangerous flame, magical effect, or volatile substance unattended.

Drakthar does not forbid violence. A warrior may kill an enemy, a soldier may burn an enemy fortress, and a smith may melt a treasured weapon down. What matters is whether the destruction serves a legitimate purpose and whether unnecessary harm was allowed to occur.

# Divine Skills
### Crafting
Drakthar's primary Divine Skill is **Crafting**.

His followers are encouraged to understand how things are made, maintained, repaired, and improved. A well-maintained sword, properly constructed bridge, reinforced door, and carefully designed furnace are all considered acts of devotion.

### Athletics
Drakthar's secondary association is **Athletics**.

The Ember Warden values physical strength when it is placed in service of protection.

# Favored Weapon - Warhammer
The warhammer represents Drakthar's dual nature with how it is simultaneously a weapon and a tool.

His priests often explain that the same hammer that breaks an enemy's armor can shape a piece of iron into a shield capable of protecting someone's life. Devotees of Drakthar are commonly taught to regard their weapons as tools rather than symbols of violence.

# Divine Devotion
Drakthar does not demand that every follower become a priest or warrior.

His religion recognizes several levels of devotion.

## Embered
Anyone who sincerely follows Drakthar's teachings may call themselves **Embered**.

At this stage, devotion provides primarily spiritual and cultural benefits. An Embered follower learns the **Ember's Vigil**, a simple ritual for maintaining a protective flame. Once per day, while maintaining a flame for at least ten minutes, the follower may designate a creature, structure, or location as being under their protection until the flame is extinguished.

This has no inherent combat effect, but many divine abilities later interact with things placed under one's protection.

## Kindled
A **Kindled** follower has demonstrated meaningful commitment to Drakthar.

They gain the following benefit:
**Warden's Vigil.** When a creature within your reach is damaged by an attack or effect, you may use your Reaction to reduce the damage by an amount determined by your level. If you reduce the damage to 0, a brief burst of harmless embers surrounds you and the protected creature. You may use this ability once per short rest.

## Blazing
A **Blazing** follower has demonstrated exceptional devotion.

They gain:
**Hearth Within.** You gain resistance to fire damage. Additionally, whenever you are within 10 feet of a creature you have designated as being under your protection, you gain a small bonus equal to half your proficiency to saving throws against effects that would cause you to become frightened, restrained, stunned, or incapacitated.

# Divine Blessings
A character may receive one through exceptional service, divine favor, or a significant act performed according to Drakthar's principles.

###### **Emberheart**
- Your body possesses a small supernatural warmth.
- You can withstand extreme cold without suffering its normal effects, and you can ignite a small flame by touching a flammable object.
- You always know whether a fire within 30 feet of you is controlled or unstable.

###### **Warden's Hand**
Once per day, when a creature within your reach would take damage, you may use your Reaction to move up to half your Speed toward it and place yourself between the creature and the source of the damage. If you reach the creature, you become the target instead.

###### **Forge-Blessed**
- Items you personally craft or repair possess unusual durability.
- A weapon, piece of armor, tool, or other significant object you create receives a minor supernatural enhancement determined by the DM.
- Such objects are often recognizable by the faint warmth they retain even after being removed from a forge.

###### **Unquenchable**
- Once per day, when you would be reduced to 0 hit points, you may instead remain standing with 1 hit point.
- Your body becomes wreathed in harmless embers until the beginning of your next turn.
- Once this gift activates, it cannot activate again until you complete a long rest.

---

# Major Divine Blessings
These gifts represent Drakthar personally taking notice of a follower.

###### **The Eternal Hearth**
- You gain immunity to environmental effects caused by extreme heat and resistance to fire damage.
- Additionally, you may produce a magical flame that cannot be extinguished by mundane means in place of effects that create normal fire.
- These flames provides warmth and light but does not burn creatures unless you deliberately command it to.

##### **Shield of the Warden**
Once per long rest, you may invoke Drakthar when a creature you have sworn to protect would suffer a potentially lethal effect.
- A massive spectral shield manifests between the creature and danger.
- The protected creature automatically succeeds on the triggering saving throw or instead takes half damage if the effect does not normally allow a saving throw.
- The shield disappears immediately afterward.

###### **Heart of the Forge**
Your body becomes partially suffused with divine flame.
- You gain resistance to fire damage and may add a small amount of fire damage to your weapon attacks equal to your level.
- Furthermore, you cannot be disarmed of an item you are actively using unless you willingly release it.

---

# Divine Curses
Drakthar's curses are generally directed toward negligence and abandonment.

###### **Cold Hearth**
The warmth seems to leave you.
- You suffer increased difficulty functioning in extreme cold and cannot benefit from magical sources of warmth unless another creature willingly provides them.

###### **Cracked Shield**
- Whenever you willingly abandon someone under your protection, your ability to protect others weakens.
- Until you meaningfully atone, you cannot use your other abilities specifically intended to protect another creature.

###### **Wildfire**
The flame within you becomes unstable.
- Whenever you deliberately cause unnecessary destruction, supernatural embers begin accumulating around you. These embers make stealth and concealment increasingly difficult until you extinguish the consequences of your actions or receive atonement. You gain disadvantage on stealth.

# Religious Practices
### The Ember Vigil
The most common ritual of Drakthar's faith.

At sunset, the Embered Faithful gather around a communal fire and inspect the things that protect their community. Weapons are sharpened, doors are repaired, fires are checked, food is prepared, damaged tools are repaired, etcetera.

The ritual typically ends when every participant can answer one question:
> **“What have I protected today?”**

---

# Offerings
Drakthar's followers traditionally offer useful things rather than valuable things.

A beautifully crafted hammer, a perfectly balanced sword, a repaired shield, or a tool made for someone who cannot afford one are considered greater offerings than gold.
