# Generics
Astrali are generally separated into different tiers based on the concept upon which they are based. Furthermore, there can be multiple Astrali with similar concepts and meanings, but this does not mean they belong to the same category as one another.

For example, there may exist various Astrali for "Justice" or something along those lines. A lower category Astrali could be of "Heroic Justice" versus the entirety of "Justice." As there are various forms of "Justice," there are various Astrali for it.

Furthermore, while it is known that Astrali originate from the [Astral Deep](Astral%20Deep), the study of Astrali and their origins has led to a few revelations regarding the Astral Deep, such as how, while initially believed to only connect to the [Aether](Aether), it actually connects to all the [Planes of Essentia](Plane%20Homepage).

### Taking Down An Astrali
While it is not impossible to take down an Astrali, what this results in differs from the tiers of Astrali (seen below). The main separation is that Eidolons and above become a sort of core when "killed," whereas Nascents fizzle out and disappear, leaving little behind. Granted, it is not yet known if this is true for Nulls and Primordials, but it is assumed to be the case.

Eventually, the cores give way to the Astrali that it once was. However, the Astrali does not always come back the same as it was before. For example, a common result of remade Astrali is losing a portion of the memories they once had. Other times, there have been shifts in their abilities. It is assumed that this relates to their concept. The volatility is higher for those of lower tiers, such as some Eidolons changing entirely.

These cores come in what is usually a spherical shape with additional details based on the concept of the Astrali and its visual appearance. No known methods have been found to destroy these cores, nor to prevent the reformation of Astrali from them.

# Codes
[[The Third Accord]] is noted to have organized Astrali with codes in their facility[^1]. The classification rules[^2] were formatted and discussed below. It is to be noted that there are occasional additions to a code, such as a lowercase letter being assigned to sub-Astrali. 
`XXX-XX-XXXX`

#### 1 - Category and Danger
**Category.** The first two characters in the code are for the first letter of the Astrali Category.

**Danger.** This is then followed by a two-character number that represents the deadliness of the Astrali, with higher numbers being more deadly. Originally, the Third Accord had left this a single character number to be used to represent how "friendly" (thus easy to research) an Astrali was; this was changed shortly before the fall of [Forn](Forn). It is assumed that this was a result of Forn pressuring the Third Accord to work on military applications of their research.

A second reason this danger rating was created is also as a result of Astrali categories having been proven to be unreliable as an indicator of how much one must worry over the Astrali, considering how early into Astrali research the categories were made. Now, this danger rating is not a rating of raw strength, but more so the attention required by the Third Accord, thus not being completely accurate for non-researchers. Difficulty in containment will also increase an Astrali's danger rating. While there are no public papers on Astrali above a danger of 09, below are some explanations to provide further context to the ratings:
$_{\text{Note that these explanations may not be completely accurate for all Astrali of its tier.}}$
1. Unless there is an extreme edge case, there should be no casualties to standard researchers.
2. While there is danger, these Astrali can be clearly dealt with and do not have large means of damage, such as lacking supernatural means of damage.
3. At this level, there is now a warranted, genuine risk for casualties. These Astrali could be dealt with without many casualties, if any at all, but tend to have unavoidable abilities as well.
4. A real danger is presented to researchers at this level if they are unprepared or lack the necessary information to deal with these Astrali. It is from these Astrali and up that researchers will lose the ability to brute force without casualties.
5. From this rating and on, Astrali have gimmicks that researchers must constantly confront during an encounter. Only by doing this can casualties be minimized, but still guaranteed. Of course, Astrali that are lacking gimmicks but of great strength and size can still be placed here.
6. Astrali here can begin to affect the psyche of researchers. As a result, researchers of higher seniority are required not to fall victim to these Astrali. Otherwise, it is a continued increase in damage potential.
7. It is at this point that Astrali begin to become major threats overall. Despite still being manageable, a good number of researchers are expected to lose their lives in the process.
8. Should a confrontation begin, it will be nigh impossible to face Astrali of this tier without losing researchers, with some Astrali of this tier even requiring sacrifices as part of their gimmicks. If the rules of these Astrali are not to be followed, an even greater danger is expected to occur.
9. There is very little that can be done about these Astrali. Rather than considering ways to "beat" the Astrali, it is simply better to consider ways to minimize losses to the Astrali.

#### 2 - Shape and Will
These characters came about around the same time as the switch from friendliness to danger classification was made. The first is used to describe the general shape of the Astrali, with the second being used for the "will" of the Astrali. An Astrali's "will" refers to how much of a toll its respective [[Gifts]] or [[Extractions]] will take on a user, with a higher number representing a higher toll on a user.
```
1 = Humanoid Form
2 = Animalistic
3 = Otherworldly or religious
4 = Inanimate objects
5 = Machinery and complex objects
6 = Abstract
7 = Structure
8 = Phenomena
9 = Tools
```

#### 3 - Unique Identifier
The last four characters given to an Astrali's classification are all numbers to give them unique numbers to avoid having any two Astrali share the same exact code.

# Tiers
### TIER Ø — Null (Nulls)
Any and all references to this category are redacted. Very little is known beyond their existence and the occasional code found for an Astrali of this category.

### TIER I — Primordial (Primordials)
Primordials are some of the most absolute of the Astrali, embodying concepts that are universal, unavoidable, and assumed to be nigh invariant across even [Kaleidica](Kaleidica). They are the embodiments of their concepts, in every context in which that concept may exist. A Primordial cannot be diminished, replaced, or meaningfully opposed, as doing so would require the negation of the underlying principle of said Primordial. They rarely manifest in discrete forms, and when they do, such manifestations are understood to be partial expressions, avatars, so to speak, rather than the true presence of the Primordial. There are theories amongst the Third Accord on whether these Primordials predate the Astral Deep or vice versa. It is currently understood that many do not, but it has yet to be proven if all Primordials came after the conception of the Astral Deep.

### TIER II — Axiom (Axioms)
Going down a tier, Axioms also represent foundational rules. Unlike Primordials, however, this strain of Astrali may vary in expression across even planes, yet their underlying function remains consistent. Many fundamentals, such as planar laws, natural constants, and metaphysical constraints, are attributed to these Axioms as this level of Astrali still dictates how reality operates rather than the fact that it operates.

### TIER III — Logos (Logi)
Logos-Tier Astrali governs matters akin to meaning, interpretation, and structure as perceived by conscious beings. It is at this level that Astrali begins to no longer enforce "rules" in reality, but instead shape how those rules are understood, remembered, and contextualized. Logi in the past have been influential in the development of civilizations, belief systems, and historical narratives, often becoming the unseen architects of fate, tradition, or doctrine. It is also this tier of Astrali that is most commonly worshipped as if they were gods, as their effects are tangible and personal. Though this does not mean that other tiers of Astrali have not seen worship throughout the ages, such as Primordials.

### TIER IV — Eidolon (Eidolons)
Eidolons are Astrali formed from recurrent experiential patterns, particularly those reinforced by mortal behavior and emotion. Unlike higher tiers, Eidolons are much more reactive and mutable, often shaped by culture, conflict, and collective belief. They may grow strong as a result of their concept or weaken when their conceptual relevance fades. Many Eidolons display a form of cyclical rebirth as time goes on, as well as instability, making them unpredictable from a research standpoint. It is also to be noted, if not elsewhere, that lower-tier Astrali tend to roam planes, especially [Materia](Materia), in forms that can be interacted with and confused for a fellow mortal.

### TIER V — Nascent (Nascents)
Nascents are most usually either incomplete or emergent Astrali, representing concepts still in the process of formation. Do note, there can also be fully formed Astrali that do not meet the criteria for a higher tier and are left in this one. Regardless, they are frequently volatile and contradictory. Some arise naturally from the Astral Deep as new ideas gain a sort of "metaphysical" weight, while others appear to be artificially induced through extreme Essentia saturation via methods unknown. The majority of Nascents fail to stabilize fully and either disappear or leave this tier. Failures tend to collapse into the Astral Deep or have a small chance to mutate into Eidolons under still unknown conditions.

# Gifts and Extractions
Both Gifts and Extractions are items or things from an Astrali that hold some effect, usually a benefit in relation to the Astrali's capabilities. Now, while these items can be tremendously useful, they do come with risks. The most notable of these risks is [Erosion](Astrali%20Homepage#Erosion), which is detailed further below. Overall, Gifts and Extractions also provide a smaller code (that being only Category and Danger) that is similar to their parent Astrali's, with a notable difference being that they do not necessarily share the same danger classification of their Astrali of origin.

The main difference between Extractions and Gifts is that Gifts are highly likely to be stronger than an Extraction from the same Astrali, beyond the fact that one can achieve a complete sync with a Gift. However, Gifts are typically much more difficult to obtain compared to the two, as they require that both the Astrali themself give the Gift and that the recipient is of the right qualities to receive it[^3]. As for Extractions, the Third Accord was the first to create Extractions, with many in the modern day attempting to do the same, to a significantly lesser degree. The only real "successful" copy would be those made by [The Janitor Association](The%20Janitor%20Association.md).

Finally, it is to be noted that these items are seemingly malleable in nature, with Gifts being able to be altered to fit the user and the manufacturers being able to set the form of Extractions, albeit to a more limited degree. Regardless of this malleability, both Extractions and Gifts always, in some way, take a physical form in relation to the Astrali, such as features like color, shape, and general design. They also do not stray much from their original purpose, though this applies more to Extractions than Gifts.

For example, an Astrali with a crossbow-based Extraction will never stray far from being a crossbow. It could be made into a smaller crossbow or a different kind of crossbow, but a crossbow it remains.

#### Accord Extractions
The Third Accord categorized their Extractions as Stable or Variable. Additionally, they had also made their Extractions in one of two forms: Offensive Extractions and Defensive Extractions, with offensive ones usually being weapons and defensive ones being worn clothing or armor. Typically, though, Defensive Extractions were given to workers in the form of complete sets, rather than mixing and matching the Extractions of many Astrali, which led to an increased rate of erosion.
- Stable Extractions: This category of Extractions was the first to be made, and as the name indicates, causes less erosion than their future counterparts. The original goal of this was to provide gear that could be equipped to just about anyone to assist the researchers of the Third Accord. However, this also results in them being the overall weaker of the two types. Despite this, many of these Extractions still exist and are given to lower-level workers due to the controlled risk.
- Variable Extractions: Variable Extractions are seen as the modern form, while they are seen as riskier to use, this is a result of being able to reach a much higher sync rate than that of its predecessor, with greater ease as well. However, this does not mean they will inherently lead to greater levels of erosion than Stable Extractions, as the sync rate can be lowered to a much more stable level, unlike the Stable Extractions and their harder-to-change sync levels. Another difference between Stable and Variable Extractions is the fact that these can and eventually will affect the body of the user (causing them to gain physical traits similar to the Astrali the gear came from), low sync rate or not. These physical side effects are still under investigation.

#### Janitor Extractions
Referring to Extractions made by the Janitor Association, these are often lower quality than the ones created by the Third Accord. Unlike the originals, these extractions are not only more volatile (and thus causing more erosion) but also come with a learning curve, rather than the natural ease-of-use that comes with those made by the Third Accord. Additionally, these Extractions can be rented out or bought from the Janitor Association itself. However, unlike the Third Accord, these get sold independently of one another and are treated separately. For example, twin daggers would need to be bought separately, and a chest piece would be sold separately from its accompanying leggings.
 
# Erosion
Mentioned in the [codes](Astrali%20Homepage#2%20-%20Shape%20and%20Will) for Astrali, each of them comes with varying levels of will. It is this will that then gets imposed upon the user in a process referred to as **Erosion**. To be more specific, these items are not exactly separated from the Astrali, but rather are more akin to borrowing bits of them. As such, those who use these can be considered syncing up with the Astrali of origin. It is specifically this syncing that causes erosion, with full erosion resulting in the Astrali's psyche writing itself over the user's. This process of getting their mind replaced by the Astrali also results in a complete or almost complete physical transformation of the user as they enter a state that is stated to be similar to that of a [[All Things Wretches|Wretch]]. This state is when a user is considered Eroded, which allows the voice and mindset of an Astrali to take over[^4].

Of course, the speed of erosion also takes into account the individual's mental state, as it is their general ability to fend off erosion. Now, it is to be noted that using items derived from Astrali takes a toll on the user's mental health, which can cause someone to erode in the middle of a long fight if that someone is not managing their usage properly. Now, this is not the only case since, as the name implies, erosion was first seen in Stable Extractions as the Extraction itself will seemingly slowly wear down a user over time, causing rather subtle changes to their personality that can be seen as harmless initially. These changes are never permanent, however, as a lowering of sync rate or complete removal of the Extraction can be used to deal with these effects.
