#Rules 
Below are the damage types used within my campaigns with a description and potato example given.

# Official
**Acid.** The corrosive, dissolving power of caustic substances.
	The dumb potato is dropped into a vat of acid. It fizzles violently, shrinks, and dissolves into a brown, gritty sludge at the bottom of the vat.
**Bludgeoning.** Pure, blunt-force trauma from impact, crushing, or falling.
	You take a warhammer and bring it down on the clueless potato while its guard is dropped. The result is a flattened disc, a fine spray of starch coating the floor.
**Cold.** The damage of freezing temperatures, ice, and hypothermia.
	You cast a freezing spell on the angry potato. It instantly hardens into a rock-solid, icy brick. As the internal moisture freezes and expands, the skin splits open with a loud crack.
**Fire.** The damage of heat, flame, and burns.
	You toss the sad potato into a roaring campfire. The skin blackens and chars into ash. Eventually, it crumbles into a smokey pile of carbon.
**Force.** Raw, magical kinetic energy. A "magic punch," if you will.
	You hit the hungry potato with a Magic Missile. There is no burn, no freeze, and no cut. The potato violently implodes from the center out.
**Lightning.** Electrical discharge and arc flash.
	You jab two copper wires into the sleepy potato and channel a lightning bolt through it. It instantly cooks from the inside out.
**Necrotic.** Negative energy that decays living matter, drains life, and induces rot.
	You cast Chill Touch on the lively potato. In seconds, it shrivels, turns pitch-black, and oozes a foul liquid. It crumbles into organic waste.
**Piercing.** Damage done by a sharp, pointed object that punctures or impales.
	You fire a crossbow bolt straight through the newborn potato. It leaves a clean, round, cylindrical hole bored through the middle.
**Poison.** Toxins, venom, and contaminants that sicken and destroy.
	You inject that joyful potato with concentrated wyvern venom. The flesh immediately takes on a sickly yellow-green hue, turns slimy, and begins to weep a watery ooze.
**Psychic.** Mental anguish, psychic overload, and the pain of the mind.
	A mind flayer targets the dumb potato with Mind Blast. While the potato has no brain to scream, the psychic assault disrupts its cellular regeneration. It rots from the inside out.
**Radiant.** Holy, searing light and positive/divine energy that burns like the sun.
	You strike the sinful potato with a Guiding Bolt. It is bathed in blinding light. The outside turns a glowing, bleached white, and the internal starches instantly superheat, killing it.
**Slashing.** Damage from a sharp, bladed edge that cuts or cleaves.
	You swing a longsword through the sturdy potato. It is neatly bisected into two perfectly smooth, equal halves.
**Thunder.** Concussive sonic force.
	A Thunderwave spell goes off right next to the deaf potato. The vibrations cause it to burst from the inside. In an instant, it is pulverized into a fluffy, starchy cloud.

# Homebrew
**Cosmic.** The damage of raw creation.
	A primordial gestures, and the heretical potato is subjected to the gravitational sheer of a collapsing neutron star. It spaghettifies into an infinitely thin strand before returning to normal a split-second later. Something is missing.
**Radiation.** The destabilization of cells and atoms.
	You bask the wandering potato in a brilliant, radiation-filled blue light. It stays cool to the touch but, within seconds, the potato swells unevenly before crumbling into a pile of warm, glowing goop.
**Identity.** Damage that erases, overwrites, or fragments the inherent "truth" of something.
	A famous potato is hit with a concentrated shard of the Noesis. The potato remains physically untouched. However, it forgets it is a potato. Its structure becomes confused as this thing... What was the example?

# Merged Types
Certain spells, monster attacks, and environmental hazards deal merged damage, which is when a single harmful effect combines two distinct damage types. A merged damage type is always denoted by its two constituent types, such as acid fire or cold thunder.

When you take merged damage, your resistances and immunities apply to the effect as a whole, but the interaction depends on how many of its constituent types you are protected against.
- **Full Protection.** If you have resistance to both constituent types, the damage is halved as normal. If you have immunity to both, you take no damage. If you have immunity to one type and resistance to another, it will count as having resistance to both.
- **Partial Protection.** If you have resistance or immunity to only one of the two constituent types, your defense is diminished by the mingled nature of the attack:
    - Resistance to one type reduces the total merged damage by _one-quarter_ (rather than by half).
    - Immunity to one type reduces the total merged damage by _half_ (rather than negating it entirely).

For example, if you are resistant to fire but take merged fire and acid damage, you reduce the total damage by one-quarter, taking three-quarters of the damage total. If you are immune to fire but not acid, you reduce the total damage by half, taking the remaining half. Only by being protected against both do you enjoy the full effect of your resistances/defensive traits.
