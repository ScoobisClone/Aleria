#Rules 
Deployable tools are traps or other mechanisms that a character may employ in dire times. They are placed down within an unoccupied 5-ft reach by spending your Bonus Action during your turn and have a trigger according to its properties, as shown below their name, much like weapon properties. If a Tool requires a Saving Throw to be passed, it will be displayed before it’s properties, with the DC’s associated Ability Score being placed in parentheses, and below that it’s effect(s) when trigger. Tools usually occupy a 5-ft by 5-ft square. You may also spend your Bonus Action to retrieve a Tool. Unless specified, all Deployable Tools only trigger against creatures hostile to who deployed it'll Tools have a Durability Property, which denominates how many times they may be activated until they break down and require repairs. A broken down Tool may be repaired by a blacksmith for half of it’s buying price or during a Long Rest spending one use of a Repair Kit during a Long Rest, where at least one Character must spend their time repairing or re-stocking the Tool. Otherwise, a Player Character with a number of levels in the Artificer Class may fix up to their Proficiency Modifier’s worth of Tools during a Long Rest. To do so, they must meet a minimum level requirement according to the Tool’s rarity, as follows:

|Artificer Level|Maximum Rarity|
|---|---|
|1|Common|
|3|Uncommon|
|8|Rare|
|12|Very Rare|
|16|Legendary|

Furthermore, Artificers may choose to use their Spell Save DC instead of the Deployable Tool’s when it is triggered, instead of the Tool’s original Saving Throw required. This also applies to the Artificer’s Spell Attack Modifier in the case the Deployable Tool performs attacks.

# Properties of Deployable Tools
**Active.** An Active Deployable Tool requires active usage from a creature who must spend their Action while within 5-ft of the Tool to trigger it.

**Adherent.** An Adherent Deployable Tool is one that can easily cling to any even surface, being able to be placed on unoccupied ceilings or walls.

**Arcane.** An Arcane Deployable Tool is one commonly used by scholars and people attuned to the arcane, such as Wizards and Sorcerers. Characters with levels in these classes may repair up to their Proficiency Bonus’ worth of Reliquary Tools during a Long Rest as if they were an Artificer, also being able to choose using their own Spell Save DC and Spell Attack Modifier for these Tools.

**Bulky.** A Bulky Deployable Tool requires more space than usual for its operation. This space is listed in parentheses after this property is listed.

**Contact.** A Contact Deployable Tool triggers whenever a creature moves into the area it occupies.

**Concealed.** A Concealed Tool is made hard to perceive. All Creatures have Disadvantage on Perception Checks to find it, with the tool having a Passive Stealth Score displayed in parenthesis following this property.

**Construct.** A Construct Deployable Tool has the same Resistances as most of its kind, being a mechanical mess of machinery in its operation. It has Resistance to acid damage, Immunity to poison and psychic damage and the Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone and Incapacitated Conditions.

**Cumbersome.** A Cumbersome Deployable Tool is particularly harder to deploy. You must instead spend an Action to deploy this Deployable Tool.

**Durability.** A Deployable Tool’s Durability measures how many activations a Tool can handle before needing repairs or re-stocking. This value displayed in parenthesis after this property.

**Far-Mount.** A Far-Mount Deployable Tool can be easily tossed and assemble itself. You may deploy a Far-Mountable Tool on any unoccupied space within a radius of yourself as shown by the number of feet within parentheses following this property’s listing.

**Instant.** An Instant Deployable Tool is one that does not keep functioning in the field as long as it still has Durability, instead having it’s Durability consumed whenever it is placed down, usually because this Deployable Tool comes in sets or may be used repeatedly. After a placed Instant Tool is triggered, it is destroyed, with only it’s pieces remaining for posterior repairs or resupply.  

**Mount.** A Mount Deployable Tool may be mounted by a creature standing in the same space as it by them spending their Action or Bonus Action to do so. While mounted their movement speed is 0 and the Bonus Modifier gained from their Dexterity Modifier on Dexterity saving throws can only be a maximum of 2. A creature may spend a Free Action to dismount. Mount Deployable Tools usually require a mounted creature's Action to be used.

**Multiple.** A Multiple Deployable Tool is actually a set of Tools that may be placed down multiple times while it still has Durability, such as Caltrops being thrown from a bag.

**Primal.** A Primal Deployable Tool is one commonly used by those who are surrounded with nature, such as Druids and Rangers. Characters with levels in these classes may repair up to their Proficiency Bonus’ worth of Primal Tools during a Long Rest as if they were an Artificer, also being able to choose using their own Spell Save DC and Spell Attack Modifier for these Tools.

**Reliquary.** A Reliquary Deployable Tool is one commonly used by churches, such as Clerics and Paladins. Characters with levels in these classes may repair up to their Proficiency Bonus’ worth of Reliquary Tools during a Long Rest as if they were an Artificer, also being able to choose using their own Spell Save DC and Spell Attack Modifier for these Tools.

**Sensor.** A Sensor Deployable Tool triggers whenever a creature of your choice moves in or outside of the range that follows this property in parenthesis.

**Sturdy.** A Sturdy Deployable Tool is unlike others, having no Durability and instead possessing it’s own AC and Maximum Hit Points, displayed respectively in parenthesis following this property. They are usually Immune to psychic and poison damage, Vulnerable to Acid damage and are Immune to the Charmed, Frightened, Grappled, Poisoned and Prone Conditions, unless stated otherwise.

**Timed.** A Timed Deployable Tool triggers on a timer, usually at Initiative Count 1 or at whatever count that is lower than yours when it has been set. If you have none, you may choose from 15 below. The Trap only triggers after any other creature with the same Initiative Count has acted.
