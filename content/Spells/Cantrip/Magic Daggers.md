# Magic Daggers
> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Level** 0 (Conjuration) · **Casting Time** 1 action · **Range** 60 feet
> **Components** V, S
> **Duration** Instantaneous
## Description
With a flourish, you conjure a throwing dagger of magical force out of thin air and flick it from your wrist at a target you can see. Make a ranged spell attack roll against a creature within range. On a hit, the target takes 1d6 force damage. The dagger vanishes after the attack.

At higher levels, you conjure more daggers out of force and make additional attacks: two daggers at 5th level, three daggers at 11th level, and four daggers at 17th level. You can use the daggers to attack the same target or different ones. Make a separate attack roll for each dagger.
