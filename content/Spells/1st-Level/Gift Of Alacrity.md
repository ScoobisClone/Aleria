# Gift Of Alacrity
> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Level** 1 (Divination) · **Casting Time** 1 minute · **Range** Touch
> **Components** V, S
> **Duration** 8 hours
## Description
You touch a willing creature. For the duration, the target can add 1d8 to its initiative rolls.
