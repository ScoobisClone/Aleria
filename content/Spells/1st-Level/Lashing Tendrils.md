# Lashing Tendrils
> [!info] Quick Reference
> **Level** 1 (Transmutation) · **Casting Time** 1 action · **Range** Self
> **Components** V, S, M (the tip of an octopus tentacle)
> **Duration** Concentration, up to 1 minute
## Description
You sprout grotesque, whipping tendrils which automatically attack nearby creatures. When a creature comes within 5 feet of you or begins its turn there, it must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

**At Higher Levels.** When you cast this spell using a spell slot of 2nd level or higher, the damage increases by 1d6 for each slot level above 1st, up to a maximum of 6d6.
