#Rules
# Introduction
This system replaces the Fighting Style class feature and brings in the 2024 editions Weapon Masteries to create a flexible Mastery Point economy, giving martial characters much more tactical depth and abilities to allow them more options. It introduces three distinct categories for a player to spend their points on:
- Combat Stances
- Martial Masteries
- Simple Masteries
- Weapon Masteries

# Mastery Points
### Gaining Mastery Points
Whenever a class feature would grant you a Fighting Style, you instead gain 1 Mastery Point. Beyond that, you gain Mastery Points based on the Mastery Points that would normally be gained by your class under the 2024 edition's class rules. For homebrew classes, discuss with your DM in regards to how many Mastery Points your class receives. 

You may also gain additional Mastery Points at the DM's discretion as quest rewards, boons, or extensive training during downtime.

### Spending Mastery Points
You may spend Mastery Points to learn abilities from the following categories:

| Category            | Cost                            | Limit                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Combat Stance**   | 1 Point per Stance              | Maximum known = your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1)         |
| **Martial Mastery** | 1 Point per tier                | Requires level 5 (Focus), 11 (Specialization), 17 (Ascendance) |
| **Simple Mastery**  | 1 Point buys 3 Simple Masteries | Maximum prepared = your Proficiency Bonus                      |
| **Weapon Mastery**  | 1 Point per Passive/Flourish    | No limit, Flourishes require the corresponding Passives        |

### Retraining
During a Long Rest, you may retrain your Simple Masteries amongst the ones you have learned up to the maximum of your Proficiency Bonus.

# Combat Stances
Combat Stances are passive combat postures that shape your approach to battle. You are not in a stance by default, you must adopt one.

### Adopting a Stance
- At the start of your turn, or as a free action on your turn (once per turn), you may adopt a Combat Stance you know or switch to a different one.
- You may only benefit from one Stance at a time.
- If you are incapacitated, you drop out of your stance.
- If you do not choose to switch, you remain in your previously active stance.

### Stance List
**Archery Stance**  
- You gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls you make with ranged weapons.
- While wielding a ranged weapon, you do not suffer disadvantage from being within 5 feet of a hostile creature.

**Berserker Stance**
- When you make a melee weapon attack, you gain a +2 bonus to attack rolls, but you suffer a -2 penalty to your AC.
- While below half your maximum hit points, this bonus increases to +4 as well as the negative to -4.

**Controlled Shot Stance**
- When you hit with a two-handed ranged weapon, you can roll one additional damage die but must then choose one damage die to remove.
- Additionally, while wielding a two-handed ranged weapon, you can ignore Half Cover when making ranged attacks.

**Defense Stance**  
- While wearing armor, you gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Class.
- When you take the Dodge action, you may also make one weapon attack as part of that action.

**Dueling Stance** 
- When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon.
- You have advantage on ability checks made to disarm an opponent.

**Great Weapon Stance**  
- When you roll a 1 or 2 on a damage die for an attack you make with a melee weapon that you are wielding with two hands, you can reroll the die and must use the new roll, even if the new roll is a 1 or a 2.
- When you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a two-handed weapon, you may make one additional weapon attack as a bonus action.

**Protection Stance**  
- When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack roll.
- You must be wielding a shield to gain this benefit.

**Two-Weapon Stance**  
- When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can add your ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.
- You can draw or stow two one-handed weapons when you would normally be able to draw or stow only one.

**Blind Fighting Stance**  
- You have blindsight with a range of 10 feet.
- Within that range, you can see anything that isn't behind total cover, even if you're blinded or in darkness.

**Interception Stance**  
- When a creature you can see hits a target, other than you, within 5 feet of you with an attack, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage the target takes by 1d10 + your proficiency bonus (to a minimum of 0 damage).
- You must be wielding a shield or a simple or martial weapon to gain this benefit.

**Thrown Weapon Stance**  
- You can draw a weapon that has the thrown property as part of the attack you make with the weapon.
- When you hit with a ranged attack using a thrown weapon, you gain a +2 bonus to the damage roll.

**Unarmed Stance**  
- Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 + your Strength modifier bludgeoning damage.
- When you make an unarmed strike, you can choose to deal bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage (representing different striking techniques).

**Mariner Stance**  
- While you are not wearing heavy armor or using a shield, you gain a climbing speed and a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.
- You gain a +1 bonus to your AC.

**Tunnel Fighter Stance**  
- As a bonus action, you can enter a defensive stance that lasts until the start of your next turn. While in this stance, you can make opportunity attacks without using your reaction.
- You can also make an opportunity attack against a creature that moves more than 5 feet while within your reach.

**Relocator Stance**
- Whenever you hit with a weapon that has the Reach property, you may move the target up to 5-ft. closer to you if they were within 10-ft. of you or push them away if they were within 5-ft. of you.
- When you move a creature in this way, opportunity attacks made against that creature by your allies have advantage until the start of your next turn.

# Martial Masteries
Martial Masteries represent advanced tactical maneuvers that you can develop to great mastery. Each Martial Mastery has a basic effect and three tiers of improvement: Focus, Specialization, and Ascendance. Each Martial Mastery applies to the same set of weapons as its Weapon Mastery of origin from the 2024 rules.

### Acquiring and Upgrading Combat Masteries
- You may spend 1 Mastery Point to learn the basic version of a Martial Mastery
- To unlock a higher tier of a Martial Mastery, you must spend an additional Mastery Point for each tier and meet the level prerequisite: 
	- Focus: 1 additional point, requires character level 5, and the basic mastery.
	- Specialization: 1 additional point, requires character level 11, and the Focus.
	- Ascendance: 1 additional point. Requires character level 17, and the specialization.

### Using Martial Masteries
- When you make a weapon attack, you may apply one Martial Mastery you know to that attack.
- You apply the highest tier you have purchased for that Mastery. 
- You may use a Martial Mastery only once per turn unless otherwise stated.
**Saving throw DC:** If a Martial Mastery requires a saving throw, the DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your choice of Strength or Dexterity modifier.

### Martial Mastery List
#### Cleave
**Basic.** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can attempt to cleave through to another creature. Make an additional attack roll against a different creature within your reach. On a hit, the second creature takes damage equal to your ability modifier (minimum 1).

**Focus: Great Cleave.** When you use Cleave, you add your full ability modifier to the damage of the second attack.

**Specialization: Whirlwind Attack.** When you use Cleave, you may target all creatures within your reach. Each target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take damage equal to your weapon damage die + your ability modifier. On a success, they take half damage. You cannot use this on the same turn you use Cleave's normal effect.

**Ascendance: Great Whirlwind.** Your reach increases by 5 feet when using Whirlwind Attack. Additionally, creatures that fail their saving throw against your Whirlwind Attack are pushed 5 feet away from you.

#### Vex
**Basic.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, the creature has disadvantage on its next attack roll against you before the start of your next turn.

**Focus: Frustrate.** If you miss a creature with an attack, that creature has disadvantage on its next attack roll against you before the start of your next turn.

**Specialization: Serpent Strike.** If you draw your weapon as part of your attack, you add your proficiency bonus to the damage roll. This applies even if the attack misses (but deals no damage).

**Ascendance: Puncture.** Your attacks with this weapon ignore any effect that reduces the damage they deal, including damage resistance, damage reduction, and effects like the _blade ward_ spell.

#### Nick
**Basic.** When you engage in Two-Weapon Fighting, you can make the additional attack of your offhand weapon as part of your Attack action instead of as a bonus action. This extra attack still follows the rules of Two-Weapon Fighting. You can only use this once per turn. 

**Focus: Quick Change.** When you make the additional attack from Nick, you can draw or stow one weapon as part of that attack. Additionally, you can make the Nick attack with either of the weapons you are wielding.

**Specialization: Combination.** When you make the additional attack from Nick, you can apply one Combat Mastery you know to that attack. You cannot use this feature again until the start of your next turn.

**Ascendance: Rend.** If you hit with the additional attack from Nick, you may make one further attack with the same weapon against a third target within your reach (once per turn). This attack also doesn't add your ability modifier to the damage roll.

#### Topple
**Basic.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can attempt to knock it prone. The creature must make a Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

**Focus: Slam.** If you successfully knock a creature prone with Topple, you may make a bonus action attack against that creature. This attack doesn't add your ability modifier to the damage roll.

**Specialization: Cranial Impact.** When a creature you have knocked prone with Topple stands up, it must make a Constitution saving throw or be Dazed until the end of its next turn. A Dazed creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and can take either an action or a bonus action on its turn, not both.

**Ascendance: Trample.** When you use Slam, you may make an unarmed strike as part of that bonus action. You can add your ability modifier to this unarmed strike's damage.

#### Push
**Basic.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can push it up to 10 feet away from you.

**Focus: Bully.** When you use Push, you may also attempt to trip the target. The target must make a Strength saving throw or fall prone in the space where it ends its movement.

**Specialization: Charge.** After pushing a creature with Push, you may move up to half your speed as a bonus action. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks from the creature you pushed.

**Ascendance: Pounce.** When you use Charge, you may make a weapon attack as part of that bonus action. If you move at least 10 feet before making this attack, you have advantage on the attack roll.

#### Sap
**Basic.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, it has disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of your next turn.

**Focus: Fatigue.** When you hit a creature with Sap, it must make a Wisdom saving throw or lose its reaction until the start of your next turn.

**Specialization: Debilitate.** When a creature is affected by your Sap, it has disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes before the start of your next turn.

**Ascendance: Concuss.** If a creature fails its Fatigue saving throw, it is Dazed until the end of its next turn.

#### Slow
**Basic.** When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**Focus: Fumble.** When you hit a creature with Slow, it must make a Dexterity saving throw or drop one item it is holding (your choice).

**Specialization: Pin.** When you hit a creature with Slow, it must make a Strength saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn.

**Ascendance: Suppress.** When you hit a creature with Slow, it must make a Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of you until the end of its next turn. While frightened in this way, it has disadvantage on attack rolls against you.

#### Graze
**Basic.** If you miss a creature with a weapon attack, you still deal damage equal to your ability modifier (minimum 1). This damage is of the same type as the weapon's damage.

**Focus: Setup.** When you use Graze to deal damage to a creature, the next attack roll made against that creature before the start of your next turn has advantage.

**Specialization: Bleed.** When you use Graze to deal damage to a creature, it gains 2 stacks of the Bleed condition.

**Ascendance: Lacerate.** You add your proficiency bonus to the damage dealt by Graze.

# Simple Masteries
Simple Masteries are universal combat tricks available to any creature proficient with the weapon they are wielding. They represent fundamental battlefield tactics that any warrior can employ.

### Preparing Simple Masteries
- You may spend 1 Mastery Point to learn 3 Simple Masteries of your choice from the list below.
- You may only have a number of Simple Masteries prepared equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
- You may change your prepared Simple Masteries during a Long Rest.

### Action Indicators
Simple Masteries use the following text-based indicators:
- Replace Attack - Replaces one of your attacks. If you have Extra Attack, only one attack per turn can be replaced this way. 
- Full Action - Takes your entire Action.
- Movement - Uses your Movement on your turn.
- Reaction - Uses your Reaction.
- Opportunity - Can be used instead of an Opportunity Attack.
- Other descriptors are added as needed to restrict the usage of certain Simple Masteries.

### Melee Attacks
#### Power Attack _(not Finesse)_
Before you make an attack with a melee weapon, you can choose to take a -5 penalty to the attack roll and attempt to put more of your raw strength behind a powerful blow.

If the attack hits, you add 2 + your Strength modifier (minimum 1) to the attack's damage.

#### Feint **(Replace Attack)** _(Finesse)_
Before you make an attack with a finesse melee weapon that you are proficient with, you perform a feint to deceive your target.

Make an attack roll opposed by the target's Passive Insight. If you succeed, you have advantage on your next attack against that target. If you fail, you have disadvantage on the next attack roll and you cannot succeed a Feint against this target for the rest of the encounter.

#### Shove / Trip **(Replace Attack)** **(Opportunity)** _(Reach or Polearm)_
While wielding a melee weapon with the Reach property, or any Polearm weapon, you can spend one of your attacks to perform the normal Shove a Creature action without the need of a free hand. Make an **Attack roll** instead of a Strength (Athletics) check.

#### Stagger **(Replace Attack)** _(Bludgeoning)_
Before you make a melee attack with a bludgeoning weapon, you can choose to take a -5 penalty to the attack roll and try to disrupt the movement of your target while attacking.

If the target takes damage, the target's movement speed is reduced by 10 until the end of its next turn. If its speed is reduced to 0 in this way, it immediately drops prone. Until the target recovers its movement speed to full, it cannot use the Dash, Disengage, or Dodge actions.

### Melee Reactions
#### Counter **(Reaction)** _(Finesse or Parry)_
When a creature misses you with a melee attack by 10 or more, you may use your reaction to perform a single melee attack against that creature. You cannot Counter while using the Dodge action.

#### Raise Shield **(Reaction)**
If you are wielding a shield and another creature hits you with a melee attack, you may use your reaction to interpose your shield between yourself and the attack and increase your AC by 2, potentially causing the attack to miss you. Your shield is damaged in the process and suffers a -1 penalty to its AC each time you block in this fashion. If your shield's AC reaches 0, it is destroyed.

Magical shields self-repair AC equal to their enchantment bonus each long rest and may block critical hits that meet the above criteria.

#### Opportunity Grapple **(Reaction)** **(Opportunity)** _(Free Hand)_
You use one of your free hands to reach out and attempt to grapple an opponent.

Make a Grapple check (see _Player's Handbook_). If this opportunity grapple is provoked by a movement and you succeed, both you and the opponent move 5 feet in the direction the target tried to move into and the target's speed becomes 0.

#### Give Ground **(Reaction)**
When you are hit with a melee attack and you haven't moved during your last turn, you can use your reaction to move 5 feet away from the creature, reducing the damage you take by 1d6 points (minimum of 1), as long as your movement speed is greater than 0. This movement does not provoke an opportunity attack from the attacker, and the attacker may immediately follow you into the space you just left as a free action.

You cannot Give Ground while you are grappled by any creature, or knocked prone. You cannot Give Ground if you are already on, or would walk into, difficult terrain.

### Ranged Actions
#### Aim **(Movement)** _(Ranged Weapon)_
If your target hasn't moved during its previous turn, you can spend your movement to stand still, hold your breath, and aim with a ranged weapon. Until the end of this turn, your next ranged attack against that target has advantage.

Aiming requires concentration. If you maintain spells requiring Concentration, Aiming causes them to end.

### Movement Actions
#### Charge **(Movement)** **(Full Action)**
When you or a mount you ride moves at least 20 feet towards a target, you may declare a charge attack as an action. You must have a clear path towards your target, and nothing should hinder your path (such as difficult terrain). If you finish your movement within reach of your target, you may use your action to follow it with one of these options:
- **Strike.** You may make a single melee attack with advantage. The attack deals additional damage equal to the distance you charged divided by 5, up to your Strength or Constitution modifier (your choice, minimum of 1). If you are riding a mounted animal that provides the movement instead of you, you may use its Strength or Constitution instead of your own.
- **Slam.** You may perform the Shove action (see _Player's Handbook_) with advantage. If you succeed, the creature is shoved twice the normal distance, or shoved as normal then knocked prone. You may choose to move up to five feet in the direction you are shoving your target. If you fail, you provoke an attack of opportunity from your target.
- **Tackle.** You may make a single Grapple attempt with advantage. If the Grapple fails, you provoke an attack of opportunity from your target and are knocked prone. If you succeed, both you and your opponent are knocked prone and your target is subjected to the Grappled condition.

#### Drive Back **(Replace Attack)**
Using one of your Attacks, you attempt to force back a standing creature. The target must be no larger than you in size. Make a Charisma (Intimidation) check against DC 10 + the target's Wisdom (Insight) check.

If you win the contest, the target backs away from you by 5 feet. As part of this attack, you can move 5 feet in the direction of the target. If you lose the contest, you provoke an attack of opportunity from the target or it may choose to use its reaction to perform a Drive Back action against you.

#### Brace **(Movement)**
You can spend your movement to move up to half your speed to a solid object and brace yourself for additional support. This requires the use of both hands. For as long as you maintain your grip, any checks to move you or knock you prone are made with disadvantage. Furthermore, saving throws to resist being moved or knocked prone are made with advantage.

Once braced, you can maintain this effect as long as you do not release your grip or use your hands for anything else. If you are moved further than 5 feet away from the braced object, or knocked prone, the effect ends.

#### Lunge **(Replace Attack)** _(Piercing, not Heavy or Light)_
If you have advantage over your target, you can make a special melee weapon attack that extends the reach of your weapon by 5 feet. You provoke opportunity attacks from enemies within 5 feet of you.

### Special Actions
#### Body-Shield **(Movement)** _(Restrained Target)_
While a creature is Restrained or Pinned by you, you may use half your movement to make a Grapple check against that creature in order to position it in such a way that it shields you from one or more hostile creatures that you are aware of. If the creature is of your size, it provides half cover and +2 AC. If the creature is one size larger than you, it provides three-quarters cover and +5 AC.

If the creature you shield yourself with becomes stunned, paralyzed, or unconscious, when attacked make a Strength (Athletics) check at DC 15 plus 5 for each size category above yours. On a failure, you do not benefit from the cover.

# Weapon Masteries
Weapon Masteries represent the pinnacle of specialized training with a specific weapon. Each Weapon Mastery is divided into two independent components: a passive and a flourish.

### Acquiring Weapon Masteries
Both the Passive and Flourish of a weapon cost 1 point each, but you are unable to learn the Flourish without first knowing its associated Passive.

Saving Throw DC for Flourishes: If a Flourish requires a saving throw, the DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your choice of Strength or Dexterity modifier.

### Weapon Mastery List 
#### Bow
**Passive: Wind's Companion.** Drawing a bow teaches you to move with the wind and anticipate the flow of battle. While wielding a bow, your walking speed increases by 5 feet. Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to initiative rolls.

**Flourish: Piercing Shot.** You draw the bowstring to its limit, loosing an arrow with such force that it punches clean through your target. Make a ranged weapon attack against a creature within your bow's normal range. If the attack hits, you may immediately make an additional ranged attack roll against a second creature directly behind the first (within 5 feet of the line of fire and within your bow's range). This second attack uses the same attack roll result (it either hits or misses based on the second creature's AC). If it hits, the second creature takes half the damage dealt to the first creature.

#### Crossbow
**Passive: Steady Aim.** The weight and mechanical complexity of a crossbow demand a steady hand and a patient eye. While wielding a crossbow, if you have not moved during your turn, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that crossbow until the start of your next turn.

**Flourish: Executioner Bolt.** You load a specially crafted bolt and take a punishing shot at a vulnerable point. Make a ranged weapon attack against a creature within your crossbow's normal range. If the attack hits, the target must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target is Stunned until the end of your next turn. On a success, the target is not stunned, but its movement speed is halved until the end of your next turn. You cannot use this Flourish again until you finish a Short or Long Rest.

#### Daggers
**Passive: Dagger's Agility.** Daggers grant unparalleled agility and mobility, granting you an additional 10 feet of movement per turn. Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to Dexterity saving throws while wielding a dagger.

**Flourish: Bounce Back.** You throw the dagger in a way that it ricochets right back to your hand after being thrown. When you do so, perform a ranged attack roll on a target within its Thrown range, ignoring Partial Cover. Regardless of if you hit or miss, the weapon is flung back to you after hitting any surface. On a hit, the target takes an additional 1 stack of Bleed as the dagger twists on impact.

#### Gauntlets
**Passive: Heavy Gauntlet's Dukes.** A good pair of Gauntlets easily help with maintaining an overall defensive stance. While wearing gauntlets, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC and a +1 bonus to Constitution saving throws.

**Flourish: Breathless.** You deliver a particularly powerful punch to the target's lungs, beating the very air out of them. Perform a melee weapon attack against a target within reach. On a hit, they must pass a Constitution saving throw or have their movement speed halved and disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of their next turn.

#### Greataxe
**Passive: Brutal Momentum.** The greataxe is a weapon of raw, relentless power. When you hit a creature with a greataxe, you gain a +1 bonus to damage rolls with greataxe attacks against the same creature for the rest of your turn (max of +3). Additionally, when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points with a greataxe, you gain temporary hit points equal to your proficiency bonus.

**Flourish: Sunder.** You bring your greataxe down in a devastating, armor shattering blow. Perform a melee weapon attack against a creature within your reach. On a hit, the target takes damage as normal and must make a Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the target's AC is reduced by 2 until the end of your next turn. The same enemy can not be sundered twice, and you can use this Flourish a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

#### Greatsword
**Passive: Greatsword's Destruction.** A greatsword in your hands neglects the need for any shield, cutting through any attack with ease. Reduce any damage you take by 2 (this increases to 3 at 11th level, and 4 at 17th level). If the weapon has a bonus modifier to hit, add that to the damage reduction as well.

**Flourish: Wide Arc.** You swing the greatsword to cover great arcs. Perform a melee attack roll against a target within reach. The result also counts as if it were targeting any other creatures 10 feet beside the original target. When you roll damage, you do so once for all targets hit. You have disadvantage on this attack if there are no other creatures adjacent to the original target.

#### Handaxe
**Passive: Handaxe's Versatility.** The Handaxe is the perfect weapon to cover for any other arm's weakness. When you engage in Two-Weapon Fighting, you still add your full ability modifier to the damage of the Bonus Action attack (instead of half). Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with thrown handaxes.

**Flourish: Reverb.** You throw your Handaxe at any target creature within its minimum thrown reach. On a hit, you may instantly rush toward them, moving to be within 5 feet of the target. You may make one additional melee attack against that target as part of this Flourish.

#### Katana
**Passive: Katana's Airwall.** The dexterity needed to wield a Katana properly is nothing to scoff at, and its bursts of action teach a special means of defense against projectiles. Ranged attacks against you suffer a -2 negative modifier to hit. Additionally, you gain a +1 bonus to initiative rolls while the katana is sheathed.

**Flourish: Blink.** You ready your Katana and slash without giving your target a moment to perceive it. You perform a melee weapon attack against a target within reach. If your Katana is sheathed, you have Advantage on this attack and deal an additional weapon damage die on a hit. Furthermore, if able, you may move to be 5 feet behind the target. After using this Flourish, you cannot use it again against the same target for the rest of the encounter.

#### Lance
**Passive: Lance's March.** The Lance is a movement in itself, a constant that cannot be broken, granting you the ability to move through up to 20 feet of Difficult Terrain each turn without needing to spend any additional movement speed. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with a lance while mounted.

**Flourish: Slaughterline.** You prepare for a mad dash with your Lance at the forefront. Choose an empty space within 30 feet to move to. All creatures in the line required for you to move to there must pass a Dexterity saving throw or be impaled, taking your Lance's damage die + your Strength modifier and being both knocked Prone and forcibly moved into the space in front of where you end, or the closest unoccupied space instead. Creatures that succeed on the save take half damage and are not moved or knocked prone.

#### Light Hammer
**Passive: Light Hammer's Wedge.** A Light Hammer's head can easily be used to wedge out even the nastiest attack from being a hit. You gain the ability to use your Reaction to increase your AC by 2 when you would be targeted by an attack, before or after it's rolled, potentially turning a hit into a miss. You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

**Flourish: Breaker.** You may use the Hammer's head to hook and tear away your opponent's weapon with ease. Perform a melee weapon attack against a target within reach. On a hit, they must pass a Strength saving throw or have one weapon of your choice that they are holding be flung toward any space within 20 feet of them. If the target fails the save by 5 or more, the weapon is flung 30 feet instead.

#### Longsword
**Passive: Longsword's Lockstep.** The Longsword's elegant maneuvers ease into a dance of attacks in your hands. When you move before attacking on your turn, you gain a +2 bonus to your next attack roll.

**Flourish: Inescapable.** Though simple in nature, a well-used Longsword is a weapon to put fear into your foe's hearts. You perform a weapon attack roll against a target within reach. On a hit, the target must pass a Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened of you until the end of your next turn. While Frightened, the target cannot willingly move away from you, as they are paralyzed by your presence. Additionally, the target has disadvantage on attack rolls against you while Frightened.

#### Mace
**Passive: Mace's Siege.** The strength needed to wield a Mace is one to tear down anything in your path, granting you the ability to deal double any bludgeoning damage to objects and buildings. Additionally, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls against creatures wearing heavy armor or with natural armor.

**Flourish: Sundermind.** You bring down your Mace in a particularly nasty blow to the head. Perform a melee weapon attack against a creature within reach. On a hit, they must pass a Constitution saving throw or receive a negative modifier to their Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws equal to your proficiency bonus until the end of their next turn.

#### Revolver
**Passive: Revolver's Deadzone.** The Revolver is a versatile weapon, but that versatility is brought out fully only when you have a perfect vision to acknowledge multiple targets at once. You deal an additional your proficiency bonus damage whenever you first hit a different creature than your last target during your turn.

**Flourish: Fan The Hammer.** Instead of performing a single attack against a single target, you'd rather empty your Revolver and the remaining hostile roster all at once. You perform one ranged weapon attack per each remaining bullet in your chamber, as per the Recoil property. Each of these attacks must target a different creature and you don't add your Dexterity modifier to your damage rolls. You may add your proficiency bonus to the damage of each hit instead.

#### Rifle
**Passive: Rifle's Positioning.** To wield a Rifle perfectly you must learn how to keep the distance between you and any other threat. Whenever a hostile creature moves into 5 feet of you, you may spend your Reaction to quickly dash away into a 15-foot line in any direction of your choice. Opportunity Attacks triggered from this movement are made at disadvantage. You may use this reaction even if you have already used your reaction this round, but doing so consumes your next turn's reaction.

**Flourish: ADS.** You take aim to pierce only through soft tissue. Instead of performing an attack roll, all creatures within a line whose range is equal to the minimum Range of your Rifle must pass a Dexterity saving throw or be hit, taking the damage you would do with a regular weapon attack using your Rifle, or half on a success.

#### Shortsword
**Passive: Shortsword's Denial.** The Shortsword's elegant maneuvers ease into a dance of attacks in your hands, having you deal +3 bonus damage on a hit whenever you move before attacking.

**Flourish: Retake Control.** You maintain dominance through any battlefield. Perform a melee weapon attack against a target within reach. On a hit, the target is moved back 5 feet each time you hit it until the end of your turn. Additionally, if the target is moved into a wall or other solid object, it takes 1d6 bludgeoning damage per 5 feet it would have moved beyond the obstruction.

#### Shotgun
**Passive: Close Quarters Blast.** A shotgun's true power emerges at close range. While wielding a shotgun, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls and a +1 bonus to damage rolls against targets within 15 feet of you. Additionally, you ignore Half Cover when attacking targets within this range.

**Flourish: Clearing Shot.** As an action, you brace yourself and unleash a wide, devastating spray of pellets that fills a corridor. Each creature in a 15-foot cone originating from you must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failure, they take your shotgun's normal damage + your Dexterity modifier and are pushed 10 feet away from you. On a success, they take half damage and are not pushed.

#### Unarmed
**Passive: Iron Fist.** Your body is a weapon honed through countless hours of training. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d8 bludgeoning damage instead of 1 + Strength modifier, or they go up by one dice level. Additionally, when you are not wearing armor or wielding a shield, you gain a +1 bonus to your AC.

**Flourish: Pin.** You deliver a precise, punishing strike to a vital join or pressure point. Perform an unarmed attack against a creature within reach. On a hit, the target takes damage as normal and must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, the target is restrained until the start of your next turn and has disadvantage on the saving throw to break free. On a success, the target is not restrained but has its movement speed halved until the end of its next turn. You can use this Flourish a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

# Rules Interactions & Overlaps
### Stacking Masteries
You may apply one Martial Mastery and one Simple Mastery to the same attack roll provided:
1. They do not both replace the attack.
2. They are compatible with the weapon you are wielding.
3. You cannot apply more than one of each type to the same attack.

A Weapon Mastery Flourish replaces one of your attacks entirely. Therefore you cannot combine a Flourish with a Simple Mastery that has the **(Replace Attack)** tag, because both would replace the same attack. You can combine a Flourish with a Martial Mastery provided the Martial Mastery does not require a specific action type that the Flourish precludes. For example, if a Flourish makes a specific attack roll, you could apply Cleave to that attack roll.

# Feats
### Stance Adept
_Prerequisite: Intelligence 13 or higher, proficiency with at least one Combat Stance_

You have honed your ability to shift between combat postures fluidly. You gain the following benefits:
- You may switch your Combat Stance as a **bonus action** instead of a free action, and you may do so even if you are not at the start of your turn (once per turn).
- You gain one additional Combat Stance of your choice (this does not count against your maximum known).

### Mastery Savant
_Prerequisite: Intelligence 15 or higher, at least 3 Mastery Points spent_

You have a preternatural grasp of martial techniques. You gain the following benefits:
- When you spend a Mastery Point to learn Simple Masteries, you learn **4** instead of 3.
- You may change your prepared Simple Masteries during a **Short Rest** instead of only a Long Rest.
- You gain one additional Mastery Point.
